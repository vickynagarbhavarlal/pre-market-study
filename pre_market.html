FINAL MARKET VIEW

RBI day: recovery is improving, but the market needs 22,800–23,000 to prove it.

Nifty has rallied about 1% for two consecutive sessions and RSI has improved, while domestic institutions continue to absorb FII selling. However, GIFT Nifty around 22,704.5 indicates a softer opening, Brent is back near $101.5 and the rupee is around 96.42. The RBI is widely expected to hike 25 bps, but the stance and forward guidance are the real market-moving variables. For swing traders, avoid chasing the pre-policy move; watch 22,800, 23,000 and the 22,600/22,400 support structure after volatility settles.

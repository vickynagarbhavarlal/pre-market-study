TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Yesterday's rebound needs confirmation; the Fed has raised the macro hurdle.

Nifty recovered 0.43% to 23,217.60 and Bank Nifty gained 0.89%, but FIIs remained net sellers. Overnight, the Fed delivered a 25-bps hike and signalled another possible hike, while the dollar strengthened and U.S. yields stayed close to 5%. GIFT Nifty is only marginally above the prior close, so the opening signal is not a strong risk-on confirmation. Holding 23,100–23,200 keeps the rebound alive; sustained acceptance above 23,300 opens 23,375 and 23,465. A loss of 23,100 shifts focus toward 23,000 and 22,960.

Sector Radar

🟢 Banks / Financials

Relative strength

Financials led Wednesday's recovery: Axis Bank +2.24%, SBI +2.06%, HDFC Bank +0.69% and Kotak +1.27%. Bank Nifty's 0.89% gain is an important confirmation pocket.

🟢 FMCG / Defensives

Steady

Defensive groups can attract flows when global rates and crude remain volatile. Look for relative strength rather than sector-wide assumptions.

🟠 IT

Rate-sensitive

TCS fell 2.60% on Wednesday while Infosys also remained under pressure. A stronger dollar can help translation, but higher U.S. rates can weigh on valuation and demand expectations.

🟠 Energy

Mixed

Lower crude helps downstream users, while upstream energy can lose some price support. The sector is increasingly driven by the direction of supply risk and margins.

🔴 High-beta / Midcaps

Risk remains elevated

The index recovery has not eliminated the need for selectivity. Avoid assuming that every high-beta stock will follow the index rebound.

🟠 Auto

Watch oil + demand

Lower crude is helpful at the margin, but the rupee and demand conditions still matter for input costs and operating leverage.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do

Wait for daily structure

Use the daily chart for swing decisions. Look for higher lows, clean breakouts and volume confirmation rather than reacting to the opening tick.

Avoid

Chasing a marginal GIFT signal

GIFT Nifty is only modestly above Wednesday's close. Treat the opening indication as a reference, not confirmation of a new trend.

Risk

Respect macro event risk

The Fed has changed the rate backdrop. Keep position size controlled and define invalidation before entry, especially in high-beta names.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Range-to-cautious setup; 23,300 is the key confirmation level.

Wednesday showed that buyers can respond after a sharp sell-off, but Thursday begins with a tougher global rates backdrop. Watch 23,100–23,200 on the downside and 23,300–23,375 on the upside. A sustained breakout with breadth matters more than the opening gap. Until that happens, selective positioning and disciplined risk are more important than chasing index strength.

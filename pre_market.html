10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase the gap

Let the opening range form. Check whether Nifty holds 23,000 and Bank Nifty reclaims 55,500–55,800 before adding bullish exposure.

Respect higher timeframes

Start with monthly and weekly structure, then daily charts. Nifty remains below major averages; one rebound does not reverse the trend. Use the 2-hour chart to refine, not overrule, the daily setup.

Define risk first

Prefer liquid stocks with measurable earnings/order triggers and relative strength. Set invalidation first, size positions to stop distance, and avoid averaging down into broken setups or treating IPO GMP as a return forecast.

Final Market View

Positive opening indication • Fragile structure

GIFT Nifty near 23,023 points to a rebound after Monday’s 1.11% Nifty decline. Asia is firmer and US markets were mostly higher, but the index remains below key averages, India VIX is 14.77, Brent is around $110.15 and FII selling continues. Treat 23,000–23,175 as the first recovery test and 22,800–22,640 as downside risk, with 22,500 the larger support reference. Prefer selective, liquid, catalyst-backed swing setups; wait for breadth and price confirmation rather than buying the opening gap blindly.

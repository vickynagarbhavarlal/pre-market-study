Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Respect the extended move

Nifty is about 8.6% above its 30 October close. That does not force a correction, but it makes chasing distant entries less attractive. Wait for pullbacks or fresh bases with a nearby invalidation.

2 • Require breadth confirmation

Monday's headline gain came with 28 Nifty decliners. A breakout is more credible if advances broaden and sector participation improves, not just if two heavyweight banks rise.

3 • Trade catalysts, not headlines alone

Divi's and Gland show strong earnings acceleration; Data Patterns shows a weak quarter but unchanged guidance; Tata Steel shows positive PAT with weak price response. Compare numbers, guidance and reaction.

4 • Keep macro risk in the plan

Hot US CPI and a 10-year yield near 5% can pressure long-duration valuations. Crude near $92 is helpful for India, but not enough to neutralise a sharp rates shock.

5 • Use index levels for invalidation

Map entries against Nifty pivot 24,989 and S1 24,894; for banks use 59,453 and 59,165. Avoid trades where the stop is too wide relative to the next realistic resistance/support.

6 • Size for event volatility

With several Q2 results due today, reduce exposure in names reporting after the close if the gap risk is not part of the setup. Do not average a failed breakout or widen a stop.

Risk & Execution Checklist

Before the open

Recheck GIFT Nifty contract, cash-equivalent pre-open indication, USD/INR, Brent and Asia. The morning GIFT feeds showed a material mismatch; resolve it from the live exchange/broker terminal rather than assuming a gap.

First 15–30 minutes

Allow the opening range to form. Check Nifty/Bank Nifty relative strength, advance-decline breadth, sector participation and whether price accepts above or below the prior-session pivot.

Risk control

Set stop, target and maximum daily loss before entry. Reduce size around result announcements and avoid illiquid smallcaps where a stop may not execute near the intended price.

Final Market View

Constructive domestic structure, but the open needs confirmation.

Monday's Nifty close above 25,000 and Bank Nifty leadership keep the broader structure constructive. However, breadth was narrow and FII buying was almost offset by DII selling. Overnight US inflation surprised higher, pushed Treasury yields toward 5% and dragged US cash indices lower; Asian markets and US futures were firmer at the morning snapshot, while crude near $92 and USD/INR near 93.92 offer some support. GIFT Nifty feeds were inconsistent, so do not treat the futures quote as a clean gap signal. Nifty's 24,989 pivot and 24,894 first support define the near-term balance; 25,140 is the first upside test. Bank Nifty's 59,453 pivot and 60,029 R1 are the equivalent levels. Prefer selective trades in sectors with earnings and breadth confirmation, and keep position size disciplined around today's Q2 results.

TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Relief rally intact, but 22,800 is the first real test — and RBI tomorrow can change the tone.

Nifty recovered 0.60% on Monday after an eight-week losing streak. GIFT Nifty points to another positive start, while oil has moved closer to $100. Yet FII selling continues and the RBI decision is tomorrow. For swing traders, follow-through above 22,650 and then 22,800 matters more than the opening gap.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Relief rally continues, but RBI policy is the next major volatility trigger.

Tuesday starts with supportive global cues and GIFT Nifty around 22,648. Monday’s 0.60% Nifty rebound, lower oil and strong financial Q2 updates have improved sentiment after the eight-week losing streak. Still, FIIs sold another ₹4,699 Cr and the index must clear 22,800 before the rebound can be treated as a stronger swing-trading setup. With the RBI decision tomorrow and a 25-bps hike widely expected, trade the levels, keep position size controlled and watch whether domestic buying can continue absorbing foreign selling.

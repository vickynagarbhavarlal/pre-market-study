10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase the open

Let the opening range form. Nifty 22,650–22,700 and Bank Nifty 54,500 are immediate risk references; wait for hold/reclaim and breadth confirmation.

Respect higher timeframes

Start with monthly and weekly structure, then daily charts; use the 2-hour chart to refine entries. An intraday bounce alone is not a trend reversal.

Define risk first

Prioritise liquid stocks with verifiable earnings, order wins or relative strength. Set invalidation first, size to stop distance, and avoid averaging down into broken structures.

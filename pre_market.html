TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

24,000 is the line; a weak global open makes the first 30–60 minutes more important than the headline GDP number.

Nifty's Tuesday low was 23,952.55 and the index closed at 24,055.80. That puts 23,950–24,000 directly under price. A sustained break can expose 23,800 and 23,600. On the upside, 24,150–24,200 is the first recovery hurdle and 24,300–24,400 remains the larger trend-repair zone. Do not confuse strong GDP with an immediate bullish setup when crude is near $96 and global yields are rising.

⚠️ US-Iran escalation

Renewed US airstrikes on Iran and Iranian retaliation have increased fears of disruption around the Strait of Hormuz. Reuters reports Brent near $96.6. For India, a prolonged oil shock is directly negative for inflation, the rupee and corporate margins.

📉 Asia selling

MSCI Asia-Pacific ex-Japan was down about 0.8%; KOSPI opened roughly 3% lower and Nikkei about 2.2% lower. This is a broad risk-off move rather than an India-specific signal.

🏦 Rates repricing

US 10-year yield reached about 4.798% and markets are increasingly pricing a near-term Fed hike. Higher global yields reduce the relative appeal of emerging-market equities and pressure valuation multiples.

06

Sector Radar

🏦 Banks — Defensive

Bank Nifty fell 1.06% Tuesday and global yields are higher. HDFC Bank succession is the largest company-specific financial risk; watch 57,000–57,600.

💻 IT — Relative Cushion

IT can outperform in a weak domestic tape, but higher US yields and a global risk-off move can limit the benefit. Prefer relative strength over blanket sector chasing.

⛏️ Metals / Energy — Mixed

Coal India and NMDC have positive company-specific triggers, but the wider energy shock can create cross-sector volatility. Oil producers may benefit while import-heavy businesses face pressure.

07

Macro & Event Watch

🇮🇳 GDP: 7.8%

Q1 FY27 real GDP growth beat expectations. This is a genuine domestic cushion, not a reason to ignore the oil shock.

💱 Rupee / Oil

The rupee has been supported by RBI dollar selling, but Brent near $96 is a major pressure point for inflation, the current account and margins.

🇺🇸 US Jobs Ahead

The upcoming US employment report is the next major global macro catalyst. Markets are already sensitive to higher Treasury yields and Fed-rate expectations.

Scenario Map

BULL CASE

24,200 → 24,300

If the gap-down is absorbed and Nifty reclaims 24,150–24,200 with improving breadth, a relief move toward 24,300 is possible. Above 24,400 is the stronger confirmation.

BASE CASE

23,950–24,200

High-volatility range trade is the cleanest base case while oil and global yields remain elevated. Strong GDP can cushion declines but may not control intraday direction.

BEAR CASE

Below 23,950

A sustained break below Tuesday's low zone would expose 23,800 and then 23,600. Rising oil plus higher yields would strengthen the downside case.

INWEALFONEY FINAL MARKET VIEW

DEFENSIVE — protect capital first; let 24,000 decide the day.

Yesterday's institutional buying and India's 7.8% GDP are genuine positives, but today's opening shock is stronger: Brent is near $96–97, Asian markets are sharply lower, global yields are rising and GIFT Nifty is pointing to a weak start. The correct trade is to wait for price confirmation around 23,950–24,000 rather than predicting a bounce. If that zone holds, a relief move toward 24,150–24,300 can develop; if it breaks decisively, 23,800 becomes the next objective.

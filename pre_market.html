09

Swing-Trader Framework

Long setup

Only after reclaim

Prefer longs only if Nifty reclaims 23,800 and holds it with breadth. A higher-high/higher-low sequence matters more than a gap-up.

Short setup

Breakdown below 23,600

If 23,600 breaks decisively, favour relative-strength shorts with defined stops rather than trying to catch the falling index.

Risk control

Smaller size

Crude near $100 plus weak structure and geopolitical headlines is a poor environment for oversized swing positions.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Bearish until proven otherwise. 23,600 is today's battlefield.

GIFT Nifty points to a weak opening, while Brent near $100, a softer rupee and higher U.S. yields reinforce the risk-off setup. The market can bounce from 23,600, but a bounce is not a reversal. The first technical repair is 23,750–23,800; bigger confirmation remains 24,000.

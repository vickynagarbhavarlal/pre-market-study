01

Global Overnight Cues

🔴 Wall Street • broad decline

US equities sold off on Monday as technology shares led the decline. The S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59, the Nasdaq lost 2.49% to 24,509.76 and the Dow declined 1.41% to 51,608.17. The Russell 2000 also fell 1.70%, showing that weakness extended beyond large-cap technology.

🔴 Semiconductors • sharp pressure

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped about 5.3%. Nvidia fell 4.1% ahead of its quarterly results due after Tuesday's US close. Concerns around AI valuations, data-centre funding and tighter US restrictions on advanced-chip access weighed on the group.

🔴 Asia • technology-led weakness

Asian markets were lower in early trade. Japan's Nikkei was down around 1%, South Korea's Kospi about 2% and Taiwan around 1.4%; Hong Kong's Hang Seng Tech index was also under pressure. Chip-heavy markets remain sensitive to the US technology sell-off.

🟠 GIFT Nifty • flat indication

The last confirmed GIFT Nifty print was 25,299.50 at 06:24 IST, only about 8 points above Monday's Nifty cash close of 25,291.10. This points to a broadly flat opening rather than a meaningful gap. The quote is timestamped and may not reflect later trading before the cash open.

🟠 US rates and dollar

The US 10-year Treasury yield eased to around 5.12% at Monday's close, while the dollar index was near 103.58. Even with the modest yield pullback, a strong dollar and elevated borrowing costs remain a headwind for emerging-market risk appetite.

🟠 What can move markets next

Nvidia's results after Tuesday's US close are the key global technology event. US retail-sales data are also due Tuesday, followed by Federal Reserve meeting minutes on Wednesday. Guidance on AI demand, inflation and rates may drive the next global risk-on or risk-off move.

Indian Market Setup

🟢 Benchmarks • Monday close

Nifty 50 gained 146.80 points (+0.58%) to 25,291.10. Sensex advanced 512.34 points (+0.63%) to 82,104.79, while Bank Nifty rose 541.80 points (+0.89%) to 61,575.90. Financials and FMCG supported the headline indices.

🔴 Breadth • weak participation

NSE cash-market breadth was distinctly negative: 1,127 advances, 2,561 declines and 89 unchanged among 3,777 stocks. The advance-decline ratio was 0.44. Midcap 100 fell 0.51% and Smallcap 100 declined 1.10%, despite gains in the benchmarks.

🟠 India VIX • higher

India VIX closed at 15.68, up 1.16%. The reading is not extreme, but it rose alongside weak breadth. Nifty weekly expiry and the global technology sell-off can still produce sharp intraday swings.

🟠 Nifty 50 • decision levels

Monday's high was 25,337.60, low 25,172.10 and close 25,291.10. The classic pivot is 25,266.93; support levels are 25,196.27 and 25,101.43, while resistance levels are 25,361.77 and 25,432.43. Sustained acceptance above 25,362 is needed to strengthen the recovery.

🟠 Bank Nifty • relative strength

Bank Nifty traded between 61,213.20 and 61,712.80 before closing at 61,575.90. Its pivot is 61,500.63; supports are 61,288.47 and 61,001.03, with resistances at 61,788.07 and 62,000.23. The index remains close to its recent high, so watch whether it can hold above the pivot.

🟠 Opening discipline

A flat GIFT indication does not remove the overnight risk from US and Asian technology shares. The first 15–30 minutes should establish whether domestic buyers absorb that pressure and whether advances begin to broaden beyond a few heavyweight stocks.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🔴 Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles • JLR guidance cut

Monday move: Shares fell about 9.2% to ₹261. Catalyst: Jaguar Land Rover reduced its FY27 adjusted operating margin outlook to 2–3% from 4% and lowered revenue guidance to £25 billion from £26 billion. Watch: Margin recovery, China demand, restructuring costs and cash flow. JLR remains a major earnings driver for the company.

🔴 Kaynes Technology • earnings pressure

Monday move: The stock hit a lower circuit and a fresh 52-week low near ₹2,744. Catalyst: Q2 consolidated profit fell about 65% year-on-year to ₹39.8 crore; revenue rose 4.4% to ₹952.5 crore, while EBITDA margin contracted to 4.2% from 12.7%. Watch: Margin recovery, debt, cash flow and management clarification before considering a reversal.

🟢 Deepak Fertilisers • strong Q2 delivery

Monday move: Shares rose as much as 8.6% intraday. Catalyst: Q2 profit increased 84.7% year-on-year to ₹393.8 crore, revenue rose 11% to ₹3,334 crore and EBITDA margin expanded to 22.5%. Watch: Sustainability of margins and commissioning of the Dahej nitric-acid and Gopalpur TAN projects.

🔴 Pricol • sharp price reaction to demerger plan

Monday move: Shares fell about 17.8% to ₹615. Catalyst: Promoters agreed to sell a 38.51% stake in the proposed demerged Pricol Autotech to Warburg Pincus for about ₹1,663 crore, subject to the scheme and approvals. Important distinction: The stake sale relates to the future demerged automotive-electronics entity, not an outright sale of the existing Pricol parent. Track the scheme, share entitlement and open-offer terms.

🟠 Hindalco • Novelis China divestment

Catalyst: Novelis agreed to sell two China-based subsidiaries for about $265 million, with a possible additional earn-out of up to $10 million. The businesses represented around 1% of Hindalco's consolidated FY26 sales. Read-through: Portfolio simplification and cash proceeds may be positive, but the financial contribution is relatively small and completion remains subject to approvals.

Nifty holds above its 25,267 pivot and sustains above 25,361.77 with improving advances and cash-market participation. The next reference is 25,432.43. Bank Nifty should clear 61,788.07 and hold to confirm banking support.

🟠 Base • expiry range

Nifty oscillates between 25,196 and 25,362 as global technology weakness offsets domestic flows. Stock-specific earnings and dividend adjustments may create isolated moves. Avoid repeated trades in the middle of the range.

🔴 Bear • lose 25,196

A decisive break below 25,196.27 exposes 25,101.43 and then the psychological 25,000 zone. Weak breadth, a further Asian technology decline or selling in heavyweight stocks would strengthen the downside case.

09

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Demand broader confirmation

Monday's positive benchmark close came with an advance-decline ratio of only 0.44. Prefer stocks outperforming their sector and the Nifty, with improving volume and a constructive close.

2 • Respect the 25,267 pivot

Above the pivot, watch 25,362 and 25,432. Below 25,196, the setup weakens towards 25,101. Treat these as reference levels, not automatic entry signals.

3 • Bank Nifty needs follow-through

Bank Nifty's pivot is 61,500.63. A sustained move above 61,788.07 with constituent breadth would improve the long setup; a break below 61,288.47 weakens it.

4 • Separate results from price spikes

For Deepak Fertilisers, TMPV and Kaynes, compare revenue, margins, cash flow and guidance. A sharp one-day move is not proof that a new trend has formed.

5 • Avoid weak smallcap breakouts

With 2,561 declines against 1,127 advances, avoid illiquid counters and low-volume breakouts. Use defined invalidation and wait for price to hold above the breakout zone.

6 • Plan overnight event exposure

Nvidia's results can reprice global technology shares after the Indian close. Reduce positions where the stop cannot absorb a gap and avoid averaging down into an event-driven decline.

Risk & Execution Checklist

Before the open

Refresh the GIFT Nifty print, USD/INR, Brent, Asian indices and the NSE pre-open auction. Check company filings, the live F&O ban list and the correct Nifty expiry contract.

First 15–30 minutes

Watch whether Nifty accepts above or below 25,267 and whether Bank Nifty holds 61,501. Check advances, declines and heavyweight participation before taking directional exposure.

Event and corporate-action risk

Account for Nifty weekly expiry, PFC/MRF/Nile ex-dividend adjustments, the Adroit board meeting and Nvidia results after the Indian close. Do not mistake an ex-dividend price adjustment for fresh selling pressure.

Position and loss control

Define the stop before entry, size for volatility and respect the maximum daily loss. Avoid widening stops, averaging into failed breakouts or carrying oversized positions through global event risk.

Indian Market Setup

Wednesday’s selling left Nifty below 22,700. The opening cue is mildly negative; the first task is to see whether the market can defend recent support rather than assume a reversal.

Nifty 50

Close: 22,622.70, down 0.44%. Support zones: 22,550–22,500, then 22,350 and 22,180. Resistance: 22,700–22,765, then 22,850 and 23,000.

Improvement: reclaim 22,765 with broader participation. Risk: a sustained break below 22,500 may expose lower supports. Levels are reference zones, not guarantees.

Bank Nifty

Financial stocks were a drag on Wednesday. Use Wednesday’s low and closing range as the immediate reference; reclaiming the prior session’s midpoint and then high with bank breadth would improve the setup. Exact live levels should be confirmed from the official chart before placing orders.

Swing-Trader Framework

Let the first hour develop

GIFT Nifty is only an indication. Watch whether 22,500 holds, whether 22,700–22,765 is reclaimed and whether sector breadth confirms the move.

Keep the higher-timeframe lens

Start with monthly and weekly structure, then confirm on daily charts. Enter only with a defined invalidation level and position size tied to stop distance.

Respect event risk

Weekly expiry, results and high crude can create fast reversals. Avoid oversized positions, averaging down into broken setups or treating IPO GMP as a reliable forecast.

Indian Market Setup

🔴 Friday • benchmark correction

Nifty fell 155.05 points (−0.61%) to 25,144.30; Sensex declined 318.56 points (−0.39%) to 81,592.45. Bank Nifty lost 484.40 points (−0.79%) to 61,034.10. Midcap 100 slipped 0.24% and Smallcap 100 fell 0.26%. The three-session rally ended as investors booked profits.

🔴 Inflation • policy expectations reset

October retail inflation rose to 6.09%, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time in two years. This has revived discussion of a possible policy-rate increase next month. Watch yields, rate-sensitive sectors and the rupee.

🟠 Breadth • weak participation

Friday's exchange-wide breadth was negative, with roughly 765 advancing shares against 1,333 declining shares in one market snapshot. A positive open needs improving advance–decline participation to become a credible recovery.

🟠 Nifty 50 • levels

Friday's reported range was approximately 25,054–25,246; close 25,144.30. Classic pivot is near 25,148; first support around 25,050 and first resistance near 25,242. Sustaining above 25,200–25,250 improves the tone; below 25,050, 24,800–24,700 becomes important.

🟠 Bank Nifty • leadership test

Bank Nifty closed at 61,034.10, down 0.79%, underperforming Nifty. It needs to regain Friday's breakdown area and show participation from private and PSU banks together.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not buy the gap blindly

GIFT is not a trade trigger. Wait for the opening range, price acceptance and volume confirmation.

2 • Breadth must improve

A recovery led only by a few heavyweight stocks is fragile. Check advance–decline participation and sector confirmation.

3 • Respect inflation risk

October CPI above 6% changes the rate narrative. Reassess realty, autos, housing finance and other rate-sensitive names.

4 • Separate news from price action

For Sandhar and other result-related names, compare reported numbers and guidance with the market reaction.

5 • Define invalidation first

Use 25,050 and 24,800–24,700 as broad Nifty risk references. Individual stocks need their own structure and liquidity-based stops.

6 • Keep event risk sized

WPI, trade data and global technology events can increase volatility. Avoid oversized overnight positions when gap risk is not planned.

Risk & Execution Checklist

Final Market View

Positive opening cue; recovery still needs proof.

GIFT Nifty around 25,255 points to a higher start, but Friday's broad correction and 6.09% retail inflation argue against chasing the gap. Foreign buying and the November inflow reversal are supportive; higher Indian and US yields, a weaker rupee and softer Asian markets are counterweights. Nifty's first test is 25,200–25,250; 25,050 is immediate downside support, with 24,800–24,700 as the broader zone. Bank Nifty must recover participation to confirm the move. Prefer selective execution after the opening range, using breadth and price acceptance as filters. A positive open is not a guarantee of a positive close.

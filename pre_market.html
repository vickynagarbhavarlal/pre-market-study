01

Global Overnight Cues

🟠 Wall Street • mixed close, tech under pressure

Tuesday's US session diverged: Dow Jones rose 0.85% to 50,018.70 and Russell 2000 gained 0.57%, while the S&P 500 fell 0.50% to 7,201.09 and Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.48% to 23,733.54. Most non-technology shares advanced, but semiconductor and AI-linked stocks dragged the Nasdaq lower.

🔴 Asia • sharp technology-led decline

At the early Wednesday snapshot, the MSCI Asia-Pacific index excluding Japan was down around 2.3%. South Korea's Kospi had fallen as much as 5.3%, Taiwan's benchmark 5.1%, and Japan's Nikkei 225 around 2.9%. Topix was about 0.5% lower and Australia's ASX 200 down around 0.9%. Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix and regional chipmakers were among the pressure points.

🔴 AI trade • Monday's rebound loses momentum

Nvidia fell 5.9% on Tuesday after its sharp Monday surge, while semiconductor shares weakened and Anthropic slipped below its IPO price. The market is again questioning the valuation and return profile of large AI infrastructure commitments. Dell's stronger AI-server demand outlook offered a counterpoint, but did not prevent the wider technology pullback.

🟠 Trade policy • fresh tariff uncertainty

Reports that the US administration may release findings from an investigation into excess industrial capacity across 16 trading partners, including China and the European Union, have revived tariff concerns. Any proposed measures could affect global manufacturing, metals, electronics supply chains and risk appetite. Details and policy action remain uncertain.

🟢 GIFT Nifty • positive opening indication

GIFT Nifty was near 25,752 at 08:02 IST, up around 256 points or 0.99% against its previous futures reference. It stood roughly 400 points above Monday's Nifty cash close, though futures and spot are not directly interchangeable. This signals a positive start, but the sharp Asian sell-off increases the risk of an early gap being sold into.

🟠 US data • important event at 7:00 PM IST

US third-quarter GDP second estimate, corporate profits and October Personal Income and Outlays, including the PCE inflation measures, are due at 8:30 AM New York time (7:00 PM IST). The data may influence rate expectations and the dollar. US markets will be closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving, which may also reduce global liquidity around the holiday.

Indian Market Setup & Technical Levels

🔴 Benchmarks • Monday's sharp reversal

Nifty 50 fell 450.95 points (-1.75%) to 25,349.65. Sensex declined 1,483.72 points (-1.77%) to 82,435.99. Bank Nifty lost 2,049.85 points (-3.17%) to 62,585.35. Nifty and Bank Nifty both closed close to their session lows after reversing early gains. The banking sell-off was materially deeper than the broader index decline.

🔴 Breadth • decisively negative

NSE cash-market breadth recorded 1,126 advances, 2,525 declines and 104 unchanged among 3,755 stocks. The advance/decline ratio was 0.45. This was a broad-based decline rather than a fall limited to a handful of heavyweight shares. A positive opening today needs follow-through in breadth to confirm genuine demand.

🟠 India VIX • elevated at 17.70

India VIX closed at 17.70, up about 2.19% on Monday. The rise in volatility alongside a steep market decline points to increased hedging demand. Expect wider intraday swings around the opening gap; avoid using normal-day position sizes until volatility settles.

🟠 Nifty • classic pivot 25,562.03

Monday's reported high was 25,992.60, low 25,343.85 and close 25,349.65. Classic pivot: 25,562.03. Support: S1 25,131.47 and S2 24,913.28. Resistance: R1 25,780.22 and R2 26,210.78. The 25,350 area is the immediate closing reference; a sustained move above 25,562 is needed to improve the short-term structure.

🔴 Bank Nifty • classic pivot 63,441.65

Monday's high was 65,193.40, low 62,546.20 and close 62,585.35. Classic pivot: 63,441.65. Support: S1 61,689.90 and S2 60,794.45. Resistance: R1 64,337.10 and R2 66,088.85. Monday's close near the low makes 62,546–62,585 the first important downside zone; a recovery above the pivot is needed to repair the damage.

🟠 Gap-up discipline

The GIFT indication suggests a sizeable positive opening relative to Monday's cash close. The first test is whether Nifty can hold above the 25,562 pivot and whether Bank Nifty recovers towards 63,442. A gap that fades below the opening range, with weak breadth and banks underperforming, would favour caution rather than immediate dip buying.

04

Sector Radar

🟢 IT • relative outperformer

Nifty IT gained 1.69% on Monday, the strongest major sector. Consumer Durables added 0.59%, India Digital 0.55%, MidSmall IT & Telecom 0.49% and Realty 0.31%. IT's relative strength is notable, but the renewed US technology and semiconductor weakness could test it today.

🔴 Metals • weakest sector

Nifty Metal fell 3.68%, the steepest sector decline. Tata Steel was among the Nifty's biggest losers, down 8.43%, amid the APL Apollo transaction and broader concerns around industrial metals. Copper, tin and aluminium also weakened as the AI-led industrial metals rebound faded.

🔴 PSU and Private Banks

Nifty PSU Bank declined 3.25% and Private Bank fell 3.19%. Bank Nifty's 3.17% loss confirms heavy selling across the financial sector. Large private banks and PSU lenders need to stabilise before the benchmark can build a durable recovery.

🔴 Financial Services

Nifty Financial Services fell 2.49%. Axis Bank declined 3.97% and HDFC Bank 3.33%, while the broader financial complex remained under pressure. Watch whether the sector can reclaim VWAP and hold the previous session's low after the opening volatility.

🟠 Auto, Energy and Oil & Gas

Nifty Auto fell 1.03%, Energy 1.78% and Oil & Gas 2.01%. The decline in crude is a potential tailwind for downstream users and transport companies, but a weaker global growth outlook and risk-off positioning may offset the benefit.

🟠 Broader market

Nifty Midcap 100 declined 1.01% and Smallcap 100 fell 0.66%. The broader indices held up better than banks and metals, but the advance/decline ratio of 0.45 shows that selling was widespread. Prioritise stocks that demonstrate independent relative strength rather than assuming a broad recovery.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🟢 Adani Enterprises • copper supply and airport funding

Kutch Copper has signed a three-year agreement worth about $1 billion at current prices to source copper anode from recycler Attero, with planned supplies of 2,000 tonnes per month. The arrangement can diversify feedstock and reduce import dependence. Separately, Adani Airport Holdings agreed to raise ₹2,790 crore from 360 ONE Alternates. Track execution, funding costs and the ramp-up of the copper business.

🟠 Tata Steel • major downstream restructuring

Tata Steel agreed to acquire a 26.6% promoter stake in APL Apollo for about ₹15,510 crore and make an open offer for another 26% at ₹2,301.40 per share. It will also sell its tubes business and Tata Steel Colors to APL Apollo for a combined consideration of up to ₹28,000 crore. The plan aims to create a larger downstream platform, but regulatory approvals, transaction completion and capital allocation remain important.

🔴 APL Apollo Tubes • financing and dilution risk

APL Apollo fell more than 21% on Monday after the transaction announcement. The proposed acquisition of Tata Steel's tubes and colour-coated businesses is large and may require significant acquisition financing before longer-term capitalisation. The strategic scale-up could create value, but leverage, funding structure, integration and shareholder dilution concerns need clear answers.

🟢 Alembic Pharmaceuticals • USFDA approval

Received final USFDA approval for its supplemental application for Bosutinib Tablets, 400 mg, following earlier approvals for the 100 mg and 500 mg strengths. The product is used in treatment of certain forms of chronic myelogenous leukaemia. The approval expands the approved strength portfolio; commercial contribution will depend on launch timing and market competition.

🟢 Adani Power • credit-rating upgrade

CRISIL upgraded the company's rating to CRISIL AA+/Stable from AA/Stable for enhanced bank loan and non-convertible debenture facilities. The upgrade is a positive credit signal and may support financing flexibility, although it does not by itself guarantee a change in earnings or valuation.

🟠 Hyundai Motor India • price increase from 1 December

Announced a price increase across select models and trims, capped at ₹8,000, effective 1 December 2026. The company cited higher input costs and said it had absorbed part of the pressure through internal efficiencies. The move may support margins, but pricing power and demand elasticity will determine the net impact.

🟠 Lenskart Solutions • institutional block deals

Two foreign institutional investors sold around 1.27 crore shares worth approximately ₹920.58 crore at ₹725 per share on Monday. Goldman Sachs entities and Societe Generale absorbed much of the offered stock. The deal creates a near-term supply overhang, but the identity of buyers and subsequent price-volume behaviour will matter more than the headline sale alone.

🟢 Jupiter Wagons • metro manufacturing joint venture

Entered an 80:20 joint venture with South Korea's Woojin Industrial Systems to manufacture complete metro coaches and propulsion systems in India. The product scope includes train control systems, automatic doors, bogies and aluminium car-body shells. It opens a new urban-rail opportunity, but investment commitments, order wins and execution timelines will determine value creation.

🟠 PhysicsWallah and Hindalco • expansion and leadership

PhysicsWallah is establishing a digital university in Patiala, extending beyond school and competitive-exam preparation into undergraduate, postgraduate and career programmes. Hindalco reappointed Satish Pai as Managing Director and appointed Hemant Sikka as Deputy Managing Director. The first is a long-term expansion initiative; the second provides management continuity.

🟠 ABB India • global portfolio expansion

ABB agreed to acquire Ziehl-Abegg's ventilation and control technology business at an enterprise value of about $2.65 billion. The deal expands its motors, fans, HVAC and industrial cooling portfolio. Strategic fit is positive, but valuation, financing and integration will determine returns.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Treat the gap as a test, not a signal

GIFT Nifty points to a positive opening, but Asia is sharply lower and US technology shares have weakened. Let the opening range form before taking a directional position. A gap-up that cannot hold its first 15–30 minute low is a warning.

2 • Use the pivot as the balance line

Nifty 25,562 is the key intraday balance reference, with 25,780 as R1 and 25,131 as S1. For Bank Nifty, 63,442 is the pivot, 64,337 the first resistance and 61,690 the first support. These are reference levels, not guaranteed turning points.

3 • Demand banking confirmation

Bank Nifty lost 3.17% on Monday and closed near its low. A Nifty recovery without a meaningful improvement in banks may be fragile. Watch HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and major PSU banks for relative strength and VWAP behaviour.

4 • Track metals and Tata Steel separately

Metals were the weakest sector, while Tata Steel and APL Apollo face a complex strategic transaction. Do not treat a sharp fall as an automatic bargain. Assess funding, leverage, approvals and price-volume stabilisation before considering a setup.

5 • Keep event risk in view

US GDP and PCE data arrive at 7:00 PM IST, after the Indian cash close. Avoid oversized overnight positions in rate-sensitive or high-beta names if the market is likely to react to the data and the Thanksgiving liquidity break.

6 • Protect capital through volatility

With India VIX near 17.70 and a large opening gap indicated, reduce trade size if stop distances widen. Define invalidation before entry, avoid averaging down without a base and do not let a missed move turn into an impulsive chase.

Final Market View

A positive GIFT signal meets a renewed global technology sell-off.

Indian equities return from a one-day holiday with a strong gap-up indication, but Monday's domestic price action was weak: Nifty fell 1.75%, Bank Nifty dropped 3.17%, breadth was sharply negative and India VIX rose to 17.70. The GIFT reading near 25,752 suggests buyers may attempt an early recovery, yet the sell-off in Asian chip markets, renewed tariff uncertainty and weakness in US technology shares argue against chasing the opening move. Nifty needs to sustain above 25,562 and clear 25,780, while Bank Nifty must reclaim 63,442 to improve the broader setup. If the gap fades and Monday's lows break, 25,131 on Nifty and 61,690 on Bank Nifty become the next support references. The preferred stance is selective and capital-protective: wait for breadth, VWAP and banking confirmation before increasing exposure.

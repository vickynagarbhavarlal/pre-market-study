🟢 BULLISH

United Breweries

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Opening may be stable-to-positive. Don't confuse that with a reversal.

Asia is strong and GIFT Nifty is near 24,000, but Brent near $97 and renewed U.S.–Iran escalation keep India's risk-reward asymmetric. Watch 24,000 first and 24,200 next. For swing trades, prefer strength above resistance or disciplined buying near validated support—not blind gap chasing.

Do not treat the GIFT Nifty gap as a trade. Let the first price structure develop around 23,500–23,600.

Watch

Relative strength + volume

Prefer daily-chart setups with clean breakouts, expanding volume and strength versus the sector/index.

Risk

Gap fade

If oil reverses higher or global gains fade, the Indian gap can unwind quickly. Position size should reflect that risk.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Positive opening setup; 23,600 remains the line that separates recovery from stronger technical repair.

US equities, Asian markets, lower Treasury yields and easing oil create a supportive morning backdrop. Domestic institutional buying is also a cushion, although FIIs remained sellers. Nifty has recovered for four sessions but remains below important moving averages. The swing-trader's job today is not to predict the gap—it is to see whether price accepts above 23,500–23,600. If it does, momentum can expand; if the gap fades and 23,300 breaks, the recovery weakens.

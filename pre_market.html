Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not chase the index close

Monday's Nifty gain came with negative breadth. Prefer stocks showing independent relative strength rather than assuming the whole market is recovering.

2 • Respect expiry dynamics

Weekly expiry can distort intraday price action. Keep position size modest and avoid interpreting option-driven spikes as durable breakouts without cash-market confirmation.

3 • Track the 25,267 pivot

Above the pivot, the 25,362–25,432 band is the upside reference. Below 25,196, downside pressure may extend toward 25,101.

4 • Separate earnings from headlines

For Deepak Fertilisers, TMPV and other result-sensitive names, review margins, guidance and cash flow. A sharp price reaction does not by itself explain the full fundamental impact.

5 • Inflation remains a constraint

With CPI at 6.09% and WPI at 12.3%, avoid assuming rate-sensitive stocks will outperform just because crude has eased.

6 • Keep overnight risk planned

Nvidia results and global technology volatility can drive overnight gaps. Define invalidation and maximum loss before entry; do not average into failed breakouts.

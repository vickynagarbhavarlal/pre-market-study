Sector Radar

🔴 PSU Banks / Financials

PSU banks were among Monday's weakest groups, while Bank Nifty fell 1.99%. Watch whether financials stabilize near the 54,400 area.

🔴 Oil-sensitive

Airlines, paints, chemicals and other crude-intensive businesses remain exposed to elevated input costs if Brent stays above $100.

🟠 IT

Nifty IT fell only 0.26% on Monday, showing relative resilience. Stock-specific demand and acquisition catalysts remain important.

🟠 Auto / Realty / Energy

Auto, Realty and Energy all declined materially on Monday. Avoid treating a sector-wide bounce as confirmation without index stabilization.

🟢 Defence / Solar / Infrastructure

Order-flow remains active in solar and infrastructure names, including Vikram Solar and NCC. Track execution rather than headline size alone.

🟠 Metals

Metals also corrected with the broader market. Relative strength should be assessed only after the broader risk-off pressure eases.

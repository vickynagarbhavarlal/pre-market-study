Today's Scenario Map

🟢 BULLISH

Above 24,284

Sustained acceptance above Friday's high can extend recovery toward 24,400, especially with breadth and Bank Nifty confirmation.

🟠 RANGE

24,200–24,284

Market remains inside the recent range. Avoid treating a small positive opening as a breakout.

🔴 BEARISH

Below 24,000

A decisive break below major support would invalidate the immediate recovery structure.

FOR SWING TRADERS

Don't buy the opening. Buy the evidence.

A swing setup becomes more interesting if Nifty breaks 24,284–24,300, holds the zone and sector breadth confirms. Rejection there is equally useful information. There is no need to front-run a breakout merely because GIFT Nifty is positive.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 24 AUGUST 2026

Mildly positive opening. Conditional bullish setup.

Global equities ended Friday higher and GIFT Nifty indicates a positive start, while crude has eased to around $92.60. The biggest variables today are 24,284 resistance, Iran-related headlines and global yields. Above 24,284–24,300 → recovery gains credibility. Above 24,400 → stronger confirmation. Below 24,000 → caution returns.

TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Positive open is a setup — not a signal to chase. 24,000–24,200 must turn from resistance into support.

Nifty closed at 23,914.45 after testing 23,786.80 on Wednesday. A gap-up into 24,000–24,100 brings price straight into the first supply zone. Macro & Event Watch

Rupee closed 2 Sep at 94.97, supported by RBI intervention. Brent near $95–96 remains the key external risk; a move above $100 would materially worsen India's macro setup.

INWEALFONEY FINAL MARKET VIEW

RELIEF-RALLY BIAS — but 24,000–24,200 is the test, not the destination.

Thursday has a better opening setup than Wednesday, with Wall Street and Asia recovering and GIFT Nifty pointing higher. But Nifty has already lost about 1% in three sessions, remains below key moving averages, and Wednesday's ₹9,501 Cr combined FII/DII buying could not prevent the decline. Treat the open as a test of demand: sustained trade above 24,200 can unlock 24,300–24,400; rejection followed by a break of 23,800 puts 23,600 back in play.

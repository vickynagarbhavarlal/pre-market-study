01

Global Overnight Cues

🔴 Wall Street • severe technology-led fall

Friday's US session ended sharply lower. The Dow Jones fell 1.93% to 48,147.33, the S&P 500 dropped 3.70% to 6,910.39 and the Nasdaq Composite declined 5.28% to 22,617.73. The Nasdaq lost about 10% for the week, its steepest weekly fall since March 2020. The S&P 500 moved into correction territory, with AI and semiconductor valuations under pressure.

🟠 US futures • early rebound, not yet a reversal

Nasdaq 100 futures rebounded nearly 3% and S&P 500 futures gained around 2% in early Monday trade after the previous week's sharp decline. The bounce may help sentiment, but a futures recovery alone does not establish that the technology-led selloff has ended. Watch whether US cash markets can hold gains when they reopen.

🔴 Asia • weak risk appetite

South Korea's Kospi was down roughly 3% in early trade and had briefly fallen close to 4% before stabilising. Hong Kong and Chinese equities were weaker, with the Hang Seng opening around 22,168, down about 242 points. Japan's cash market was closed for a public holiday. The regional pressure remains concentrated in technology and semiconductor-linked shares.

🟢 GIFT Nifty • positive opening indication

GIFT Nifty was near 25,912 at 07:58 IST, indicating an early gap-up of about 111 points over Friday's Nifty close of 25,800.60. The indication was above Friday's cash-market high, but futures basis, overnight headlines and the pre-open auction can change the final opening level. Treat it as a reference, not a target.

🟠 AI and semiconductor risk

Investors are reassessing whether large, debt-funded data-centre investments can generate adequate returns. The previous week's steep losses in US and Asian chip names have spread beyond a handful of technology stocks. This matters for Indian IT, electronics manufacturing and high-valuation growth stocks even if domestic flows remain strong.

🟠 Global policy and event risk

Markets remain sensitive to US inflation data, Treasury yields and the expected December policy decisions. In India, the RBI's next policy decision is scheduled for 4 December; the Federal Reserve's next meeting is on 9 December. OPEC+ ministers are due to discuss 2027 production policy on 29 November.

Indian Market Setup & Technical Levels

🟢 Benchmarks • banks led Friday

Nifty 50 advanced 286.30 points (+1.12%) to 25,800.60. Sensex gained 897.96 points (+1.08%) to 83,919.71. Bank Nifty rose 1,572.20 points (+2.49%) to 64,635.20, closing just below its session high and setting a fresh 52-week high. Banking leadership remains the strongest domestic support.

🟠 Breadth • weak below the headline rally

NSE cash-market breadth recorded 1,392 advances, 2,237 declines and 113 unchanged among 3,742 securities on Friday. The advance/decline ratio was 0.62. This is a clear warning that the index rise was not matched by broad participation. Large-cap and bank strength should not be mistaken for a market-wide breakout.

🟠 India VIX • higher at 17.32

India VIX closed at 17.32, up 7.51% on Friday, despite gains in the benchmark indices. That combination points to higher expected swings and greater demand for protection. With NSE derivatives expiry brought forward to today, intraday price movement and option premiums can remain unstable.

🟠 Nifty 50 • classic pivot 25,651.53

Friday's Nifty open was 25,368.00, high 25,808.80, low 25,345.20 and close 25,800.60. Classic pivot: 25,651.53. Support: S1 25,494.27, S2 25,187.93. Resistance: R1 25,957.87, R2 26,115.13. Friday's high at 25,808.80 is the immediate breakout reference; the 26,000 zone is the next major round-number hurdle.

🟠 Bank Nifty • classic pivot 63,971.52

Friday's Bank Nifty open was 62,656.65, high 64,680.80, low 62,598.55 and close 64,635.20. Classic pivot: 63,971.52. Support: S1 63,262.23, S2 61,889.27. Resistance: R1 65,344.48, R2 66,053.77. A hold above the pivot would preserve the strong banking structure; failure below S1 would warn that Friday's sharp rally is being unwound.

🟠 Momentum • stretched near-term readings

Nifty's daily RSI was around 78 and Bank Nifty's around 83 at Friday's close, both in overbought territory. Price remains strong, but chasing an extended move into an expiry session carries poor asymmetry. A pullback that holds support is healthier than a gap-up followed by a sharp loss of momentum.

Nifty Private Bank gained 3.01%, while Nifty Bank advanced 2.49%. All 14 Bank Nifty constituents ended higher. Axis Bank, IndusInd Bank and other private lenders contributed to the move. The sector is the key test for whether the domestic rally can withstand weaker global sentiment.

Financial Services rose 2.02% and Realty advanced 1.71%. PSU Bank gained 1.23%. These groups helped large-cap indices outperform the broader market. Watch for follow-through buying rather than assuming a second strong session after an extended run.

🟢 IT • Friday rebound, global risk remains

Nifty IT rose 1.30% on Friday, but the sharp selloff in US technology and chip stocks is a fresh external headwind. Indian IT may show relative resilience, but high-beta technology and semiconductor-linked names need price confirmation after the open.

🟠 Auto and Pharma

Auto gained 0.65% and Pharma rose 0.05%. Maruti's price increase may support revenue realisation at the margin, while the broader auto move was modest. Pharma was nearly flat and stock-specific regulatory updates are more important than the sector headline.

🔴 Media • weakest major sector

Media fell 1.68%, the weakest among the tracked sector indices. Consumer Durables slipped 0.40%, Metal fell 0.23% and Energy declined 0.22%. These groups need to recover relative strength before they can be treated as leaders.

🟠 Broader market • lagging

Nifty Midcap 100 gained 0.66%, but Smallcap 100 fell 0.13%. With the broad cash-market advance/decline ratio at 0.62, stock selection remains more important than index direction. Avoid treating a headline index high as evidence that most stocks are participating.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🟢 APL Apollo Tubes • Tata Steel stake purchase

Tata Steel approved the acquisition of a 26.38% stake in APL Apollo Tubes for about ₹15,510.6 crore at ₹2,100 per share and a mandatory open offer for a further 26%. If fully subscribed, Tata Steel's holding could rise to about 52.38%. The transaction offers strategic control and a downstream growth opportunity; approvals, financing and final completion remain key conditions.

🟠 Tata Steel • large strategic investment

The proposed APL Apollo acquisition expands Tata Steel's exposure to value-added structural steel products and downstream distribution. The cash commitment is substantial, so investors will weigh strategic benefits against capital allocation, funding costs and execution. The open offer and regulatory approvals are still part of the process.

🔴 IndusInd Bank • former CEO terminated

The bank terminated former managing director and CEO Sumant Kathpalia's employment and invoked malus and clawback provisions following the enquiry into derivatives-accounting discrepancies. The action marks a governance step, but recovery amounts, legal process and any remaining financial or control implications will remain under scrutiny.

🟢 Waaree Energies • 700 MW solar plus storage PPA

Its subsidiary signed a power purchase agreement for a 700 MW solar project integrated with a 700 MW / 2,800 MWh battery storage system in Solapur, Maharashtra. The project expands the company's firm renewable-power pipeline; execution, financing and project returns will determine the eventual earnings contribution.

🟢 Premier Explosives • export order

Received an export order worth US$33.6 million, approximately ₹311 crore, for manufacture and supply of defence explosives, with execution scheduled over 12 months. The order adds to visibility, but delivery schedules, margins and working-capital requirements remain important.

🟢 Rail Vikas Nigam • railway workshop project

RVNL emerged as the lowest bidder for an East Coast Railway workshop expansion project valued at approximately ₹348.49 crore, including taxes, with a stated 24-month execution period. It is a positive pipeline development, but L1 status is not the same as a final work order.

🟢 Adani Energy Solutions • transmission acquisition

Agreed to acquire 100% of Vikramshila Power Transmission, which holds a transmission project linked to a 2,400 MW power-supply arrangement in Bihar. The deal expands its transmission portfolio; completion conditions and project execution remain the next milestones.

🟠 Welspun Living • up to ₹3,200 crore capex

The board approved capital expenditure through its subsidiary for a research and development centre, terry-towel capacity expansion and additional spinning and weaving integration. The plan may strengthen scale and integration over time, but its size, funding and execution period make return on capital the key monitorable.

🟠 Maruti Suzuki • selective price increase

Raised prices across select models by a weighted average of around 0.31%, effective 22 November, citing sustained cost pressures. The change can partly offset input costs, but the overall increase is modest; demand elasticity and model-wise pricing will determine the margin impact.

🟢 Ashoka Buildcon • Jaipur Metro L1 bid

A joint venture with SAM India emerged as the lowest bidder for Jaipur Metro Phase II projects valued at approximately ₹2,758 crore. The potential order is meaningful for the project pipeline, but award confirmation, execution terms and working-capital needs should be tracked.

🟢 RailTel • AI platform order

Received an order worth approximately ₹27.03 crore for an AI-based legal-assistant platform. The win supports its digital-services pipeline, although the order is modest relative to the company's overall business and execution will determine revenue recognition.

🟢 Texmaco Rail • wagon and crossing orders

Received two letters of intent worth a combined ₹230.58 crore for 553 wagons, along with a separate ₹5.74 crore order for weldable CMS crossings. The orders add to visibility; the LOIs should be distinguished from completed execution and cash collection.

🟢 Berger Paints • industrial flooring acquisition

Approved the acquisition of 100% of Rocland Private Limited for consideration of up to ₹18.8 crore, expanding its presence in industrial flooring. The ticket size is small relative to the parent; integration and incremental revenue contribution are the key tests.

🔴 Natco Pharma • USFDA Form 483 observations

The USFDA inspection of its Kothur formulation facility concluded with seven observations in Form 483. The company described the observations as procedural in nature. The response and subsequent regulatory assessment matter more than the number of observations alone; avoid assuming the matter is closed.

🟠 Cohance Life Sciences • VAI classification

The USFDA completed an inspection of its finished-dosage facility and issued a Voluntary Action Indicated classification. VAI is preferable to an official-action classification, but the company still needs to address observations and maintain compliance.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Separate gap-up from trend confirmation

GIFT Nifty points to a positive start, but Friday's US selloff and weak Asian markets create conflicting cues. Let the opening range form; do not chase the first candle simply because the pre-open indication is higher.

2 • Respect the NSE expiry shift

Nifty weekly and NSE monthly contracts expire today, not Tuesday. Review all November index and stock F&O positions, physical-settlement obligations and margin requirements. Avoid leaving unintended positions into the close.

3 • Use index pivots as decision levels

Nifty pivot 25,651.53, S1 25,494.27 and R1 25,957.87; Bank Nifty pivot 63,971.52, S1 63,262.23 and R1 65,344.48. A level matters only when price action, volume and breadth confirm it.

4 • Track breadth beyond the banks

Friday's A/D ratio was only 0.62 despite the benchmark rally. Prefer stocks making higher highs with supportive volume and sector strength; avoid broad dip-buying where the underlying market remains weak.

5 • Respect stretched momentum

Nifty and Bank Nifty RSI readings were elevated and India VIX rose to 17.32. Reduce position size when volatility expands, avoid oversized short options and do not average down without a defined invalidation point.

6 • Trade news with execution context

Distinguish an awarded order from an L1 bid, a signed PPA from commissioned capacity, and a proposed acquisition from a completed transaction. Price the likely earnings impact instead of reacting to headline size alone.

Final Market View

Positive domestic opening cue, but breadth and global risk argue against aggressive chasing.

Friday's 1.12% Nifty rise and 2.49% Bank Nifty gain, combined with strong FII cash buying and a firmer rupee, leave the domestic trend constructive. GIFT Nifty near 25,912 at 07:58 IST suggests a positive start. Yet Wall Street's technology-led correction, weak Asian markets, Friday's 0.62 advance/decline ratio and a 7.51% jump in India VIX show that the rally is not risk-free. The key Nifty references are 25,809 on the upside, the pivot at 25,651.53, first support at 25,494.27 and deeper support at 25,187.93. Bank Nifty needs to hold 63,971.52 to preserve its leadership. With NSE derivatives expiry today and the exchanges shut tomorrow, intraday swings and position adjustments may be amplified. The preferred stance is selective, smaller-sized and confirmation-led: let price, breadth and banking participation validate the opening move before increasing exposure.

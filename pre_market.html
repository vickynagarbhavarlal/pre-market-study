FINAL MARKET VIEW

Relief rally likely. Trend reversal not proven.

Friday has the ingredients for a stronger opening: Wall Street rallied, US yields eased and GIFT Nifty points higher. But India still has to absorb expensive crude, geopolitical uncertainty and renewed FII selling. The cleanest framework is to watch whether 24,000–24,200 becomes support or remains supply.

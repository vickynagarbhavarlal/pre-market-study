Banking is cushioning the market; IT is the weak link.

Stock-Specific News Radar

Only company-specific triggers are included here; generic sector commentary is kept separate.

🟢 BULLISH

Bharat Electronics

Received additional orders worth ₹730 Cr since Aug 10 across communications, radar, avionics, tank sub-systems, cybersecurity, EVMs, jammers and other electronics. This adds order visibility and reinforces the defence-electronics backlog narrative.

🟢 BULLISH

Juniper Green Energy

Q1 FY27 revenue rose 81.2% YoY to ₹291.2 Cr and net profit rose 54.4% to ₹33.5 Cr; EBITDA rose 89.4%. The growth is strong, though investors should also watch whether the unusually high margin profile sustains.

🟢 BULLISH / WATCH

Mankind Pharma

Approved absorption of Bharat Serums and Vaccines' business on a going-concern basis. The transaction deepens the company's hospital/specialty healthcare footprint; integration and economics are the next questions.

🟢 BULLISH / EVENT

Fino Payments Bank

RBI approved extension of Ketan Merchant's tenure as Interim CEO for three months from Aug 27 or until the regular MD & CEO takes charge. Removes an immediate leadership uncertainty, though it is not an earnings catalyst.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

ICICI Prudential AMC

Prudential Corporation Holdings is reported to be considering a 2% stake sale to meet minimum public-shareholding norms, with an indicated offer size around ₹3,121 Cr. Potential supply overhang matters more than fundamentals here.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Foseco India

Promoter entities Morganite Crucible and Morgan Terrassen are reported likely to sell their entire 15.27% stake via a block deal at a floor price of ₹5,773.63. The transaction creates near-term supply but may improve public float.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Aye Finance

Alpha Wave India I is reported likely to sell its 7.8% stake through a block deal with an indicated floor price of ₹167. This is primarily an ownership/liquidity event.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

IRB Infrastructure Developers

Approved investment of up to ₹351 Cr in IRB InvIT Fund through a preferential issue. The transaction is linked to funding acquisitions of two project SPVs and is strategically relevant to the group structure.

🟠 NEUTRAL / EVENT

Vedanta

Vedanta Resources denied media speculation about selling its stake in Vedanta or Vedanta Aluminium. The clarification removes one immediate uncertainty, while the larger demerger/value-unlocking plan remains the main structural story.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Max Healthcare

Subsidiary Alps Hospital received a DGGI show-cause-cum-demand notice involving ₹165.7 Cr, including ₹110.47 Cr penalty. It is a material regulatory/tax overhang until resolved.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Tata Power

A Singapore court dismissed the company's challenge to an arbitral award; Tata Power plans to appeal. The legal outcome keeps an adverse litigation overhang alive.

🔴 BEARISH / WATCH

UPL

Group COO Toshan Tamhane is resigning effective Aug 31. This is primarily a management-continuity event; the market will watch for any broader leadership or execution implications.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

G R Infraprojects

Received a ₹322 Cr tax demand from the Indore tax authority. The company may contest it, but the notice is a near-term legal/tax overhang.

Today's Scenario Map

🟢 BULLISH RECOVERY

Hold 24,150 + reclaim 24,313

If the support zone holds and Nifty reclaims 24,313, the first objective is 24,350–24,400. Above that, momentum can extend toward 24,480–24,500.

🟠 RANGE / CHOP

24,150–24,313

This is the most likely noise zone if banks remain firm but IT remains weak. Avoid treating every 50-point move as a trend.

🔴 BEARISH

Break below 24,150

A clean loss of 24,150–24,190 invalidates the immediate recovery setup and shifts focus toward 24,050–24,080.

FOR SWING / INTRADAY TRADERS

Wait for the first 30 minutes to reveal whether the gap is real.

The overnight Nvidia/oil combination is positive, but Wednesday's Nifty close was technically weak. The disciplined trade framework is to watch 24,150 support and 24,313 resistance. If price opens strong but fails to hold above 24,313, don't chase. If support breaks, don't average blindly. Bank Nifty strength is the confirmation variable that can change the picture.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 27 AUGUST 2026

Cautiously constructive — but 24,150 is non-negotiable.

Nvidia's strong outlook, better Asian sentiment and lower crude improve the opening backdrop. Yet Nifty closed Wednesday at its day low and below 24,300. Above 24,313 → recovery attempt. 24,150–24,313 → range/chop. Below 24,150 → bearish continuation toward 24,050–24,080. Bank Nifty holding 57,650–57,700 would materially improve the bullish case.

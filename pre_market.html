Received a Karnataka GST show-cause notice involving an alleged ₹48.59 Cr excess-input-tax-credit demand for FY2022–23. It is a notice, not a final liability, but creates a legal/tax overhang.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Do not buy weakness blindly. The index is still in a downtrend.

GIFT Nifty is near 23,809, Brent is above $97 and Nifty remains below key moving averages. The better swing-trader framework is to sell failed rallies into 23,900–24,000 unless price sustains above 24,023. For longs, 23,606–23,721 is the zone to watch for a genuine reversal signal, not a blind dip-buy.

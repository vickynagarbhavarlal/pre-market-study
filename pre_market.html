Global Overnight Cues

🔴 Wall Street • technology rout deepens

Friday's US session saw a sharp AI and semiconductor-led selloff. S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910.39, Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.28% to 22,617.73 and Dow Jones lost 1.93% to 48,147.33. The S&P entered correction territory, more than 10% below its October record. Technology valuation concerns have spread beyond a handful of stocks.

🔴 Asia • uneven start, risk remains elevated

At 06:28 IST, South Korea's Kospi was down 3.02% to 4,722.75. Australia's ASX 200 was marginally higher by 0.10%, while Hang Seng futures indicated a weaker Hong Kong open. Japan's markets are closed for a public holiday. Regional volatility remains elevated after Friday's US selloff.

🟠 GIFT Nifty • positive opening indication

GIFT Nifty was around 25,913 at 06:12 IST, about 112 points above Friday's Nifty cash close. This suggests a positive start, not a confirmed trend reversal. Futures can change before 09:15 IST; track the pre-open auction and first 30-minute price action.

🟠 US rates and risk appetite

The US 10-year Treasury yield was last indicated near 4.91%, lower than earlier levels as investors sought safety. A softer yield can support duration assets, but the equity selloff shows that risk appetite remains fragile.

🟢 Dollar and emerging markets

The rupee strengthened to around ₹92.66 per US dollar in early indications, after gaining about 1.7% last week. Foreign inflows and softer oil have supported the currency. A renewed dollar bid or risk-off outflows could quickly reverse the move.

🟠 Oil • lower but still a macro variable

Brent January futures were near $91.85 and WTI near $82.86 in early Asian trade, both down around 0.7–0.8%. Lower crude is constructive for India's import bill, but geopolitical supply risks remain.

Indian Market Setup & Technical Levels

🟢 Nifty 50 • strong close, narrow participation

Nifty closed at 25,800.60, up 286.30 points (+1.12%), after trading between 25,345.20 and 25,808.80. The close was near the session high, but market breadth was weak: 1,392 advances versus 2,237 declines across 3,742 stocks. Friday's index strength was not a broad-market confirmation.

🟢 Bank Nifty • record closing high

Bank Nifty ended at 64,635.20, up 1,572.20 points (+2.49%), after a 62,598.55–64,680.80 range. Large private banks led the move, and the index closed close to its record intraday high.

🟠 India VIX • volatility warning

India VIX rose 7.51% to 17.32 even as Nifty advanced. Rising volatility alongside index gains suggests demand for protection and caution beneath the surface. Avoid oversized positions.

🟠 Nifty • pivot 25,651.53

Friday OHLC: high 25,808.80, low 25,345.20, close 25,800.60. Classic pivot 25,651.53. Supports: S1 25,494.27 and S2 25,187.93. Resistances: R1 25,957.87 and R2 26,115.13. Friday's low at 25,345.20 is the deeper reference if the pivot fails.

🟠 Bank Nifty • pivot 63,971.52

Friday OHLC: high 64,680.80, low 62,598.55, close 64,635.20. Classic pivot 63,971.52. Supports: S1 63,262.23 and S2 61,889.27. Resistances: R1 65,344.48 and R2 66,053.77.

🟠 Opening discipline

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not chase the GIFT gap

GIFT Nifty's early premium can disappear before the cash open. Wait for the pre-open indication, opening range, VWAP and first 15–30 minutes.

2 • Treat breadth as a filter

Friday's 1,392 advances versus 2,237 declines is a warning despite the benchmark rally. Prefer stocks showing relative strength and rising volume.

3 • Respect both index pivots

Nifty 25,651.53 and Bank Nifty 63,971.52 are the session balance levels. A sustained hold supports continuation; rejection and VWAP failure call for caution.

4 • Monitor bank leadership

Private banks led Friday's rally and Bank Nifty reached a record close. If banks hold support, they may cushion volatility; if they reverse, downside can accelerate.

5 • Separate catalyst from valuation

Waaree, Berger, Indian Bank, Ashoka Buildcon and RVNL have specific developments. Size each announcement against revenue, margins, capital needs and market value.

6 • Account for Tuesday's holiday

Tuesday 24 November is a market holiday. Avoid carrying oversized leveraged positions through the non-trading day; verify settlement and expiry calendars.

Final Market View

Positive opening signal, but the market still needs breadth confirmation.

Friday's 1.12% Nifty advance and 2.49% Bank Nifty surge delivered strong headline closes, while foreign investors were net buyers. However, 2,237 stocks declined against 1,392 advances and India VIX rose to 17.32, exposing a clear divergence beneath the index. The US technology-led selloff and weak Asian markets remain meaningful external risks, even as GIFT Nifty indicated a positive start near 25,913. The key test is whether Nifty can hold 25,651.53 and broaden participation. Above that level, 25,957.87 and 26,115.13 are upside references; below it, 25,494.27 and 25,345.20 matter. Banks remain the critical leadership group. Trade selectively, size down around elevated volatility and remember that Tuesday is a market holiday.

Global Overnight Cues

🟠 Wall Street • mixed close

Wednesday's US session ended mixed. Dow Jones fell 1.11% to 50,198.17, S&P 500 slipped 0.23% to 7,323.63, while Nasdaq Composite rose 0.42% to 24,429.88. Investors weighed Nvidia's results against concerns about the AI trade and the Federal Reserve's policy outlook.

🔴 FOMC minutes • hawkish undertone

Minutes of the October 27–28 meeting showed almost all policymakers saw a need for further rate increases in coming months if inflation remains above target. The discussion strengthens the case for caution around US yields, the dollar and global equity valuations.

🟠 Asia • mixed to weaker

Asian markets were mixed early Thursday. Japan's Nikkei was up around 0.98%, while Topix was marginally lower; Hang Seng futures pointed to a weaker Hong Kong open. The regional picture offers little broad confirmation of a risk-on rebound.

🔴 GIFT Nifty • negative start

GIFT Nifty was around 25,149 at approximately 06:57 IST, a discount of nearly 90 points to the previous futures close. This points to a lower start for domestic indices. Futures quotes can move materially before the 09:15 cash open.

🟢 Crude • continued decline

Brent futures were around $91.30 per barrel, down 0.71%, while WTI was near $82.92, down 0.41% in the morning report. Lower crude is a relative positive for India's import bill and fuel-sensitive sectors, provided the decline persists.

🟠 Dollar and yields

The US dollar index was around 103.8, near a 19-month high, while Treasury yields eased after the latest US session. The combination of a firm dollar and hawkish Fed expectations can still limit risk appetite in emerging markets.

🟠 Opening discipline

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not buy a falling index blindly

Wednesday's breadth ratio of 0.40 is a warning. Wait for a higher low, reclaim of VWAP and improvement in advances before taking fresh longs.

2 • Nifty pivot is the first test

25,253 is the balance point; 25,365 is first resistance and 25,116 first support. Trade only after price confirms which side is being defended.

3 • Watch PSU banks selectively

PSU Bank was the relative outperformer. Look for individual stocks holding support with volume; do not generalise one sector's resilience to the whole market.

4 • Avoid weak cyclical catch-ups

Metals, Auto and Consumer Durables saw sharp selling. A one-session bounce is not a reversal; wait for structure, relative strength and volume confirmation.

5 • Separate stock catalysts from index risk

Jio Financial, NALCO and Bharat Forge have company-specific developments. Evaluate the actual price reaction and liquidity instead of chasing the headline.

6 • Protect capital

Reduce position size when breadth is poor, define stops before entry and avoid averaging down. Keep overnight exposure proportionate to the risk from global rates and currency moves.

Final Market View

Defensive opening bias; wait for breadth to repair.

Wednesday's decline was more concerning for its breadth than its headline percentage: 2,581 declines against 1,025 advances shows selling was widespread. Nifty closed at 25,227.95 and GIFT Nifty near 25,149 points to another weak start. The 25,253 pivot and 25,365 resistance are the first recovery hurdles; 25,116 is immediate support, followed by 25,005. Bank Nifty's 62,056 pivot and 61,748 support deserve close attention. Lower crude and strong FII buying are constructive, but DII selling, a firm dollar and hawkish Fed minutes complicate the picture. Prefer capital preservation and selective setups until the index reclaims key levels and market breadth improves. Do not mistake a technical bounce for a confirmed trend reversal.

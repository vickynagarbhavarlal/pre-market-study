Today's Scenario Map

🟢 BULLISH RECOVERY

Reclaim 24,200

If the gap-down is absorbed and Nifty reclaims 24,200, watch 24,284–24,300. A sustained move above 24,300 is the real confirmation.

🟠 CHOPPY

24,000–24,200

Likely two-way trade. Avoid forcing a directional view while price remains below the recovery hurdle.

🔴 BEARISH

Below 24,000

A clean break can reopen downside momentum. The next reference becomes 23,850 and lower support zones.

FOR SWING TRADERS

Today's edge is patience, not prediction.

With GIFT Nifty pointing to a weak open, the easy mistake is to short the gap without waiting for price discovery. The better framework is simple: see whether 24,000 holds, whether breadth improves, and whether Nifty can reclaim 24,200. If not, stay defensive.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 25 AUGUST 2026

Weak opening cue. Defensive bias until 24,200 is reclaimed.

GIFT Nifty is signalling a lower start while US tech and Asian markets are soft. Lower crude is a partial positive, but Iran-related uncertainty and global risk appetite remain the bigger variables. Above 24,200 → stabilisation. Above 24,284–24,300 → recovery confirmation. Below 24,000 → bearish pressure increases.

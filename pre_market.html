Global Overnight Cues

🟠 Wall Street • mixed finish

Wednesday's US session ended unevenly. Dow Jones fell 1.11% to 50,198.17, S&P 500 slipped 0.23% to 7,323.63, while Nasdaq Composite gained 0.42% to 24,429.88. The technology-heavy index held up better, but the broader market remained under pressure.

🔴 Federal Reserve • hawkish minutes

Minutes of the October 27–28 meeting showed almost all policymakers believed further rate increases could be needed if the economy evolved as expected. A couple of officials said a 50-basis-point move could be considered if incoming inflation data remained unfavourable. The next meeting is scheduled for 8–9 December.

🟠 Asia • mixed, Hong Kong weaker

Early Thursday trading was mixed. Japan's Nikkei was around 59,060, marginally higher, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng was down about 1.76%. The broader Asia-Pacific market outside Japan was little changed, offering no strong regional risk-on signal.

🔴 GIFT Nifty • lower opening indication

GIFT Nifty was at 25,175 at 08:24 IST, down 47 points from its previous futures close of 25,222. This is roughly 53 points below Wednesday's Nifty cash close and indicates a cautious start. Futures can move sharply before the 09:15 IST cash open.

🟠 Crude • stabilising after a fall

Brent was around $92.12 per barrel, up 0.18%, while WTI was near $83.17, down 0.11%, at about 08:07 IST. Both had fallen in the previous session. Higher US inventories and expectations of a supply surplus are weighing on prices, while shipping risks in the Strait of Hormuz remain a source of volatility.

🟠 Global rates and AI listing

The US 10-year Treasury yield eased 3 basis points to 5.07% on Wednesday; the 2-year held at 4.80%. Separately, Anthropic priced its Nasdaq IPO at $83 per share, with trading expected to begin today. The debut will be watched as a test of demand for large AI-related listings amid recent technology-sector volatility.

🟢 NALCO • critical-mineral opportunity

Signed an MoU with RUSAL to study recovery of scandium from red mud, an aluminium-production by-product. The collaboration offers strategic technology and resource-recovery potential, but commercial scale, economics and timelines are yet to be established.

🟢 BLS Polymers • strong Q2 growth

Q2 FY27 revenue rose 28.92% year on year to ₹125.56 crore, EBITDA increased 92.98% to ₹17.31 crore and profit after tax grew 130.63% to ₹10.54 crore. Operating leverage and margin expansion are constructive; check liquidity and the stock's price response.

🟠 Jio Financial • JV capital infusion

Jio Financial Services and BlackRock each subscribed to ₹123 crore of new shares in their 50:50 Jio BlackRock Asset Management venture through a rights issue. The funding supports operations and platform development; meaningful earnings contribution will depend on business scale-up.

🟠 IIFL Finance • Fairfax stake transfer

Fairfax's investment arm sold about 5.65 crore shares, roughly 13.24% of IIFL Finance, at ₹617 through secondary-market deals. Institutional buyers participated and the stock closed around ₹661.95, up about 4%. The sale proceeds go to the seller, not the company; monitor ownership changes and post-deal price stability.

🟠 Bharat Forge • defence subsidiary funding

Infused about ₹290.51 crore into wholly owned Kalyani Strategic Systems through a fresh share allotment funded from QIP proceeds. This strengthens the subsidiary's capital base; the market will look for deployment, order execution and returns on the investment.

🔴 Sky Gold and Diamonds • governance and dilution risk

Shareholders approved fundraising of up to ₹1,800 crore through eligible routes, including QIP. The company also disclosed ₹10.70 crore of unauthorised transfers at a subsidiary; ₹3.35 crore has been recovered and ₹7.35 crore recognised as an exceptional loss. Shares fell about 5.28% on Wednesday. Monitor governance controls, recovery and potential dilution.

🟠 PhysicsWallah • FY30 ambition

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Let the gap settle

Do not buy the first red candle or chase a gap-down recovery. Wait for the first 15–30 minutes to establish whether the opening low is defended.

2 • Use the pivot as a filter

25,253 is Nifty's balance point; 25,365 is first resistance and 25,116 first support. Prefer entries only after price confirms which side is being defended.

3 • Demand breadth confirmation

Wednesday's advance/decline ratio of 0.40 is a warning. Fresh longs need improving participation, a higher low and a reclaim of VWAP rather than a narrow index bounce.

4 • Follow relative strength, not yesterday's leaders blindly

PSU banks, Media and Pharma were relatively resilient. Check individual charts, volume and support structure; one strong sector session does not guarantee continuation.

5 • Treat company news selectively

NALCO and BLS Polymers have constructive catalysts, while Sky Gold carries a governance and dilution overhang. Confirm the price reaction and avoid chasing a headline after an extended move.

6 • Protect capital

Predefine the stop, reduce exposure when breadth is poor and avoid averaging down into a failed support. Keep overnight positions proportionate to global rate, currency and commodity risk.

Scenario Map

Relief bounce holds

Nifty reclaims 22,800–22,850 and holds with better breadth while Bank Nifty moves above 54,870. Then 23,000 becomes the next reference; look for selective relative strength rather than a broad buy-everything trade.

Range / whipsaw

Nifty remains between roughly 22,370 and 22,800 with VIX elevated and banks unable to reclaim the pivot. Avoid chasing either side; wait for a clear structure on the daily and 2-hour charts.

Support breaks

A sustained break below 22,370, accompanied by Bank Nifty below 53,940, raises the risk of a move toward Nifty 22,170–22,200 and Bank Nifty near 53,360. Reduce exposure rather than averaging down.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Let the opening range settle. A weak GIFT Nifty and elevated crude are context, not an entry signal; require a defined level reclaim or breakdown with volume and breadth.

Use higher timeframes

Start with monthly and weekly direction, refine on the daily chart, and use the 2-hour chart for entries. A single intraday bounce does not repair a damaged swing structure.

Define risk first

Prioritise liquid names with a verifiable catalyst and relative strength. Size positions to the invalidation level, avoid averaging into broken structures, and do not force a trade in a high-volatility tape.

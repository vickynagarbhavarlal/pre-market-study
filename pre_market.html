01

Global Overnight Cues

🟢 US markets

Wall Street closed higher on Monday: Dow Jones +0.28% to 52,361.58, S&P 500 +0.70% to 7,676.77 and Nasdaq +0.83% to 26,169.38. AI-linked shares rallied after Anthropic's IPO filing. The move improves risk appetite, but investors still need evidence that AI capital spending can translate into durable earnings.

🟢 Asia

Asian shares rallied early Tuesday. Japan's Nikkei was up about 1.9%–2.0%, South Korea's KOSPI gained more than 4%, and MSCI Asia-Pacific excluding Japan rose around 1.2%. The positive global impulse supports sentiment, though India's own opening indicator remained weaker and crude remains a domestic headwind.

🟠 Oil & geopolitics

Brent eased about 0.7% to $114.95/bbl and WTI to around $101.23, retreating from near six-month highs as diplomacy offered some hope. Supply risk has not disappeared: renewed disruption could push Brent back toward recent highs and quickly reprice inflation and risk assets.

02

Indian Market Setup

The Nifty rose 0.68% on Monday and recovered from an intraday low near 22,387, ending at the session high of 22,753.20. That is a useful rebound, not yet a confirmed trend reversal: the index remains below key moving averages and must clear the 22,800–22,850 area. Tuesday is monthly derivatives expiry, increasing the risk of sharp, misleading intraday moves.

Nifty 50

Close: 22,753.20 • Monday range: 22,387.35–22,753.20. Immediate support: 22,500–22,400. Pivot supports: 22,491, 22,405, then 22,265. Resistance: 22,800–22,850; pivot references 22,771, 22,857 and 22,997, then 23,200 as a broader range boundary.

A sustained hold above 22,500 and breakout through 22,850 would strengthen the recovery case. A rejection below resistance or a break of 22,400 keeps the rebound vulnerable.

Bank Nifty

Close: 54,366.70 • Monday range: 53,777.50–54,392.50. Classic pivot: 54,179. Support: 53,965, 53,564 and 53,350. Resistance: 54,580, 54,794 and 55,195. The 55,000 zone is also a major psychological and options reference.

Bank Nifty's gain was modest compared with the broader market rebound. A sustained move above 54,800–55,000 with participation from large private banks would improve the setup; losing 53,965 would weaken it.

Volatility, breadth & derivatives

India VIX closed at 17.16, up about 1.8%. Monday's rebound improved breadth, but the Nifty's broader structure is still weak. The 22,500 put and 23,000 call were notable options-interest references; open interest can shift quickly and should not be treated as guaranteed support or resistance. Monthly expiry can distort both price and option premiums.

Scenario Map

🟢 Recovery extends

If Nifty holds the 22,500–22,400 zone, reclaims 22,800–22,850 and sustains above it with broad participation, the next reference is 22,997–23,000. Metals, pharma and large banks should confirm the move; a gap-up or a single strong candle is not enough.

🟠 Expiry-day range / whipsaw

Between support near 22,400–22,500 and resistance around 22,800–22,850, expect two-way trade and failed breakouts to remain possible. Avoid overinterpreting the first move; use closing/holding behaviour and a retest before treating a break as valid.

🔴 Rebound fails

A decisive break below 22,400 exposes 22,265 and then the broader 22,200 area. Bank Nifty losing 53,965 and then 53,564 would reinforce a risk-off signal. Elevated crude and continued FII selling would add pressure, but these are conditional scenarios, not forecasts.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Strong global cues do not guarantee a strong Indian open when GIFT Nifty and crude point the other way. Let the first 30–60 minutes reveal whether price accepts above support or rejects resistance; expiry-day noise makes early breakouts less trustworthy.

Use higher timeframes

Start monthly, then weekly, then daily charts, with the 2-hour chart as the lowest timeframe for setup refinement. Prioritise relative strength versus Nifty, clean bases or breakout-retests, supportive volume and a clearly defined invalidation level.

Define risk first

Set stop-loss and position size before entry. If volatility widens the stop distance, reduce size rather than loosening the stop. Check earnings dates, liquidity and overnight-gap risk; do not buy solely because a headline or IPO GMP looks attractive.

Final Market View

Global tailwind • domestic caution • selective recovery only

Base case: a cautious, potentially volatile open. Wall Street and Asia are strong, but GIFT Nifty remained below its relevant futures reference, Brent is still near $115, the rupee is weaker and foreign institutions sold on Monday. Nifty's 0.68% rebound is constructive, but the trend is not repaired until 22,800–22,850 is reclaimed with breadth. Treat 22,500–22,400 as the immediate risk zone and keep expiry-day exposure controlled.

What changes the view? A sustained move above 22,850 with broad sector participation supports a push toward 23,000. A breakdown below 22,400, especially alongside weaker banks and renewed oil strength, would put 22,265–22,200 back in focus.

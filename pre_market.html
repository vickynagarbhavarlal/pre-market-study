TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Do not mistake the two-day bounce for a trend reversal.

Nifty recovered 0.43% on Wednesday, but the index remains well below its recent swing high and FIIs continued selling. The Fed's 25-bps hike and guidance for possible further tightening raise the bar for sustained risk-on flows. GIFT Nifty is almost flat-to-marginally positive, so there is no strong pre-open confirmation. Holding 23,100–23,200 keeps the rebound alive; above 23,300, 23,375 and 23,465 become the next upside tests. Below 23,100, the market can retest 23,000 and 22,960.

Scenario Map

🟢 BULL CASE

23,300 breaks and holds

If Nifty sustains above 23,300 after the opening noise, the next upside band is 23,375–23,465. Banks must participate for the move to have credibility.

🟠 RANGE CASE

23,100–23,300 churn

The most practical base case if global cues stay mixed. Short-covering can keep the index supported, but repeated rejection near resistance argues for patience.

🔴 BEAR CASE

23,100 fails

A sustained break below 23,100 shifts the focus to 23,000 and 22,960. High-beta and weak relative-strength names should be avoided.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do

Trade confirmation

Prioritise daily-chart structure: higher low, resistance reclaim, volume confirmation and relative strength. The first green candle is not a setup by itself.

Avoid

Chasing IPO or index gaps

Do not confuse high IPO subscription or GMP with guaranteed listing returns, and do not chase a market gap while Nifty is still below major resistance.

Risk

Keep size controlled

Fed uncertainty, oil above $100 and weekly expiry can create false breakouts. Define invalidation before entry and reduce exposure when price action becomes noisy.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Neutral-to-cautious. Let 23,300 prove the bulls.

Wednesday's bounce improved short-term sentiment, but it did not repair the larger structure. The Fed has now raised the global rate hurdle, while FIIs remain sellers and crude is still above $100. A clean reclaim of 23,300 followed by 23,375–23,465 would make the rebound more credible. Until then, the better swing-trading approach is selective exposure, not aggressive bottom-fishing.

09

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Don't chase

After an 8.6% weekly rally, opening spikes can offer poor reward-to-risk. Wait for a range and defined invalidation.

2 • Respect expiry

Monday expiry and Tuesday holiday can amplify gamma and late-session moves. Avoid oversized positions.

3 • Relative strength

Prefer stocks outperforming Nifty and sector peers, supported by volume and a disclosed catalyst.

4 • Muhurat liquidity

Sunday's short session distorts volume comparisons. Give greater weight to Monday's normal-session breadth.

5 • Risk first

Size from stop distance and fixed account risk. Never average a failed breakout or widen a stop.

6 • Holiday-aware holding

Account for settlement and cash availability before carrying positions into Tuesday's closure.

Final Market View

Trend constructive; Monday is a confirmation and expiry session.

The market has delivered a powerful weekly rally on foreign buying, easing crude and earnings. Sunday’s Muhurat close was mildly negative; GIFT Nifty around 25,043 signals a near-flat start. Asian equities are rebounding, but a stronger dollar and higher Fed-hike expectations keep macro risk alive. Treat 25,000 as the pivot: sustained acceptance above 25,050–25,100 can reopen 25,200–25,400; failure below 24,900 exposes 24,800 and 24,700. Monday expiry and Tuesday’s holiday argue for smaller positions, selective trades and strict risk limits. A short Muhurat session is not proof of a low-risk market.

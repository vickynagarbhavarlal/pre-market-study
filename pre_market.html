Final Market View

Domestic trend positive, but the opening gap and 25,500 hurdle matter.

Tuesday's advance and Bank Nifty's record high keep the domestic structure constructive. However, Nifty's failure to sustain above 25,500, near-balanced breadth and a lower GIFT indication argue against an aggressive opening stance. Nvidia's earnings beat may support global technology sentiment, but Wall Street's regular-session decline and elevated US yields remain risks. Nifty's 25,385 pivot is the immediate balance point, with 25,269 support and 25,503 resistance. Bank Nifty above 62,068 retains relative strength; 62,648 is the next upside reference. Prefer selective long setups only after opening weakness is absorbed and breadth improves. Avoid chasing the first bounce and keep risk controlled.

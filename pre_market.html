01

Global Overnight Cues

🔴 US markets • AI trade repriced

Wall Street ended sharply lower on Friday, 30 October: Dow −1.09% to 51,770.31, S&P 500 −1.82% to 7,580.07 and Nasdaq −2.86% to 25,437.00. Concerns about the scale and return on AI investment intensified after results from Intel and Amazon. Intel fell 28.58%, Nvidia 4.22%, AMD 10.88% and Broadcom 5.48%. This is a direct sentiment headwind for global technology, even though Indian IT shares outperformed locally on Friday.

🟠 Asia • mixed, not uniformly risk-off

Monday’s Asian hand-off was mixed after the US technology sell-off. Japan’s Nikkei 225 was down about 0.44% and Topix 0.60%; South Korea’s Kospi edged up 0.16% while Kosdaq slipped 0.02%. Hong Kong Hang Seng futures pointed to a higher opening. The divergence matters: pressure remains concentrated in technology and selected markets rather than showing a synchronized regional liquidation.

🟢 GIFT Nifty • gap-up indication

GIFT Nifty was around 23,298 at 06:59 IST, a premium of nearly 200 points over the Nifty futures’ previous close. This points to a firm opening after Friday’s domestic gains. The gap is only an indication, not confirmation: watch whether cash-market breadth, banks and the 23,200 zone validate the move after the open. US–Iran talks and the coming US midterm elections remain headline risks.

02

Indian Market Setup

The headline index extended its recovery on Friday, but the rally was narrow: mid-caps and small-caps declined, Bank Nifty underperformed and only 29 Nifty constituents advanced against 21 declines. A gap-up needs follow-through beyond 23,200 and broader participation before it can be treated as a durable breakout.

Nifty 50 • positive short-term structure

30 Oct OHLC: Open 22,964.50 • High 23,118.10 • Low 22,905.10 • Close 23,053.10 (+0.51%). Pivot resistances: 23,107, 23,157 and 23,238. Pivot supports: 22,944, 22,894 and 22,812. The 22,800–22,900 band remains the key demand zone; 23,200 is the immediate swing-high hurdle, with 23,500–23,600 beyond it. RSI rose to 53.5; price remained above rising 10-day and 20-day EMAs, while the 50-day average is flattening.

Bank Nifty • recovery attempt, trend still weaker

30 Oct range: 54,580.75–55,068.50 • Close 54,686.95 (−0.41%). Pivot resistances: 54,965, 55,080 and 55,266. Pivot supports: 54,592, 54,477 and 54,291. RSI was 44.97; the index remained below key moving averages and faced the 20-day EMA. A fresh MACD positive crossover is an improving signal, but the broader trend is not yet repaired. The 55,000 strike is also a major options positioning reference.

Benchmarks, volatility & breadth

Sensex closed at 74,459.53 (+0.42%). Bank Nifty fell 0.41%; Nifty Midcap 100 declined 0.93% and Smallcap 100 fell 1.85%. India VIX closed at 15.30, up about 1.2%. Broader-market breadth was weak: 1,536 advances against 3,240 declines across 5,003 traded counters; Nifty constituents were 29 up and 21 down. The index rise therefore overstates the strength of the average stock.

Crude • Currency • Yields

Brent / WTI crude
$111.65 / $102.28

Brent futures were down 1.57% and WTI 1.76% in Monday’s early trade after Iranian and Qatari officials held talks aimed at ending the US–Israeli war on Iran. The fall offers short-term relief to India’s import bill, but both benchmarks remain elevated. The improvement is constructive, but persistent oil prices and foreign portfolio outflows remain risks to the currency trend.

The US 10-year Treasury yield ended Friday near 4.94%, while India’s 10-year benchmark yield was about 7.412% on 30 October. Bond yields remain a valuation input for long-duration equities and a driver of emerging-market flows. Lower oil and a stable rupee help, but geopolitical inflation risk can reverse any yield relief.

05

Sector Radar

🟢 IT, healthcare & pharma led

Nifty IT gained 2.32%, Nifty Healthcare 1.58% and Nifty Pharma 1.55% on Friday. These sectors supplied much of the benchmark’s support. IT’s domestic strength now faces the external test of a sharp Nasdaq and semiconductor sell-off; favour stocks with company-specific earnings support and relative strength rather than buying the entire sector on a gap.

🟠 Banks and financials were mixed

Nifty Financial Services edged up 0.03%, Private Bank was nearly flat and Bank Nifty fell 0.41%. The lack of bank confirmation is important because banks carry heavy index weight. A sustained Bank Nifty move above 55,080–55,266 would improve participation; below that zone, treat the move as a recovery attempt rather than a sector breakout.

🔴 Broader cyclicals and small caps lagged

Railways fell 2.11%, Telecom 2.10%, Defence 2.01%, Capital Goods 1.91% and Consumer Durables 1.78%. Midcap 100 declined 0.93% and Smallcap 100 fell 1.85%. The divergence between IT/pharma leaders and the weak broader market argues for stock selection, tight invalidation levels and no indiscriminate dip-buying.

Scenario Map

🟢 Gap-up continuation

If Nifty holds the opening gap, clears 23,107–23,157 and sustains above 23,200 with improving breadth, the next upside zone is 23,500–23,600. Bank Nifty above 55,080 and then 55,266 would add confirmation. Require acceptance above the level, not a brief opening spike.

🟠 Range / gap-fill

If the index fails near 23,200 but holds 22,900–22,812, a wide consolidation is plausible. Positive GIFT Nifty and lower crude may cushion the market, while US technology weakness, narrow breadth and earnings gaps can produce two-way price action. Let the first hour establish whether buyers defend the gap.

🔴 Failed breakout

A sustained break below 22,812 and the 22,800 zone exposes the 22,700 put base and then the 22,600 area. Bank Nifty below 54,291 would confirm renewed banking weakness. A rebound in crude, a weaker rupee or another global technology sell-off would strengthen the downside case.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase the gap

GIFT Nifty indicates a positive start, but the market has not yet proved that the move can hold. Wait for the opening range to form and observe whether Nifty sustains above 23,000–23,107 and whether banks and broader breadth participate. A gap-up is not an entry signal by itself.

Keep the higher-timeframe sequence

Use monthly, weekly and daily charts first, with the 2-hour chart as the lowest decision timeframe. For a 3–90 day swing, prioritise relative strength, clean bases, breakout-retests, volume confirmation and a clear invalidation level. Avoid letting intraday noise override the higher-timeframe setup.

Price event and liquidity risk

Check Q2 results, monthly sales, block deals, ex-dates, IPO listings and F&O restrictions before entry. Size positions from the stop distance, not from conviction. Do not convert a positive earnings headline, order win or unofficial IPO GMP into a trade without chart confirmation and a defined exit.

Final Market View

Positive opening cue • narrow rally • confirmation still required

GIFT Nifty near 23,298 and Brent’s pullback point to a constructive start. Friday’s Nifty close at 23,053.10 and improving short-term momentum support a recovery attempt, but the evidence is not uniformly bullish: Bank Nifty fell 0.41%, mid-caps and small-caps declined, broad-market breadth was weak and India VIX rose to 15.30. FII buying of ₹3,599 Cr is encouraging, but DII selling and October’s ₹34,843 Cr FPI outflow keep the flow picture mixed. US technology weakness is another external risk.

Practical stance: stay selectively constructive only while Nifty holds 22,800–22,900. A sustained break above 23,200 with broader participation can extend the rebound; failure at resistance followed by a loss of 22,812 weakens it. Bank Nifty needs to reclaim 55,080–55,266 before it can confirm the move. Do not chase the opening gap; wait for price, volume and sector participation to agree.

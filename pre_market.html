INWEALFONEY MARKET VIEW

Relief rally possible. Trend reversal not confirmed.

Trade the levels, not the headline. A positive GIFT Nifty and strong prior-day institutional buying are constructive, but they do not cancel the damage from three consecutive declines, elevated crude and high global yields. Above 24,200, shift from defensive to cautiously constructive. Below 23,800, respect the downside.

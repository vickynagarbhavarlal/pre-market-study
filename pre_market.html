FINAL MARKET VIEW

Defensive recovery setup: 23,000 is the key support; 23,250 is the first confirmation level.

Friday's bounce from the 23,000 area is constructive, but the broader trend remains weak and the seventh consecutive weekly decline shows that sellers still control the larger structure. The overnight macro picture has also deteriorated again: Brent is back around $106, US-Iran diplomacy is uncertain, and Indian/global bond yields remain elevated. For Monday, the first task is to see whether 23,000 holds; only a sustained move through 23,250 and then 23,300 would materially improve the short-term setup.

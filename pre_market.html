10

Swing-Trader Framework

Don't chase the pre-open signal

Let the first 30–60 minutes establish the range. Check whether 22,420–22,500 is accepted or rejected and whether breadth confirms the move.

Use higher timeframes

Start with monthly and weekly structure, then validate on daily charts. Enter only with a defined invalidation level and position size based on stop distance.

Protect capital first

High oil, rising inflation, elevated yields and foreign selling favour selectivity. Avoid averaging down into broken charts; wait for acceptable reward/risk and trend confirmation.

11

Final Market View

Modest opening signal; risk backdrop remains bearish

GIFT Nifty near 22,423.5 suggests only a small premium over Monday’s close, while Asian markets are sharply lower, Brent is above $105 and Indian CPI has risen to 5.54%. Nifty needs to reclaim 22,500 and then 22,600–22,800 to improve its structure; 22,300, 22,180 and 22,000 are downside references. DII buying is cushioning continued FII selling, but that is not yet a reversal signal. Prefer selective, risk-defined trades and do not chase the opening gap.

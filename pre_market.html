Scenario Map

23,620 breaks and holds

A sustained breakout above 23,620 would strengthen the recovery and put 23,750 into focus, with short covering possible.

🟠 RANGE CASE

23,300–23,620 churn

Support holds but resistance caps the rebound. This keeps the market non-directional and favours selective swing setups.

23,270 fails

A sustained break below 23,270 can trigger fresh selling toward 23,150 and then 23,000.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Range-bound recovery with a cautious bias until Nifty clears 23,600–23,620.

Wednesday's 0.50% rebound was broad-based and supported by metals, FMCG, realty and PSU banks, while FII and DII flows were both positive. However, the overnight setup is less supportive: Wall Street weakened, Treasury yields moved toward 5.1%, and Brent returned near $100. The technical structure is improving but not repaired. For Thursday, 23,300–23,270 is the first downside decision zone and 23,600–23,620 is the upside confirmation zone.

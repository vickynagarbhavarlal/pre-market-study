🛢️ Oil shock

Reuters reports Brent crossed $91 as renewed US-Iran fighting intensified. India imports about 85% of its crude, making sustained oil strength negative for inflation, the rupee and fuel-sensitive margins.

📉 Yields surge

US 10-year Treasury yield reached 4.78%, while Japan's 10-year approached 3%. Higher global term premiums raise the hurdle rate for emerging-market equities.

🌏 Asia cautious

Nikkei was down around 0.2% and Hang Seng about 0.7% early. Friday's US jobs report remains a major global catalyst.

INWEALFONEY FINAL MARKET VIEW

Cautious to bearish below 24,200; constructive only after a sustained reclaim.

India's 7.8% Q1 growth is strong, but it does not erase the external macro shock. Respect 24,000 as the risk line and wait for 24,200–24,300 to be reclaimed before increasing long exposure. If 24,000 breaks decisively, reduce aggression and watch 23,800 first.

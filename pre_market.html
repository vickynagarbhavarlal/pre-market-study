RBI has turned hawkish: now 22,400–22,500 is the line that matters.

For swing traders, the rebound is no longer enough evidence of reversal; holding 22,400–22,500 is critical, while 22,700–22,800 is the first recovery hurdle.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

RBI has shifted the regime: protect capital until 22,800 is reclaimed.

Nifty closed at 22,603.05 after the RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% and moved to calibrated tightening. The rupee weakened toward 96.75, Brent stayed above $101 and FIIs sold ₹6,121 Cr while DIIs bought ₹4,597 Cr. The rebound is therefore not yet a confirmed reversal. For swing traders, 22,440–22,500 is the immediate line in the sand; 22,700–22,800 is the first recovery hurdle and 23,000 is the larger confirmation zone.

FII + oil + yields

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Positive opening expected. Trend reversal not confirmed.

GIFT Nifty, stronger Asian markets, positive US equities and lower Brent support a relief bounce. But Nifty’s eighth straight weekly decline, persistent FII selling and the 22,000–22,600 long-term support zone argue for discipline. Treat 22,600 as the first decision area, 22,800–22,900 as the recovery band and 22,200 as a key downside reference. Prefer strength that survives the opening rather than chasing the gap.

Global Overnight Cues

🟠 Wall Street • mixed close, technology remains fragile

Monday, 2 November: the Dow gained 0.41% to 51,983.98, while the S&P 500 fell 0.51% to 7,541.40 and the Nasdaq lost 1.07% to 25,164.32. AI-spending and funding concerns weighed on technology: Oracle fell sharply after outlining a large AI-infrastructure funding plan, while Marvell and other chip-linked names also weakened. The October US manufacturing ISM improved to 55.5, with new orders and production expanding, but the equity market is questioning how quickly AI capex converts into cash flows. This is a selective technology de-rating, not a uniform Wall Street sell-off.

Asian shares opened softer as the US chip-stock sell-off carried into the region. Scenario Map

🟢 Bull case • 23,500 breakout holds

Nifty sustains above 23,500–23,507, then clears 23,597 with broad advances and follow-through beyond Reliance. Bank Nifty reclaims 55,164 and then 55,341, confirming financial participation. If Brent holds near $105 and the rupee remains around ₹94.9, the next Nifty zones are 23,700 and 23,800–23,900. A close above resistance—not the opening tick—is the confirmation.

🟠 Base case • positive gap, then consolidation

GIFT-led strength meets supply around 23,500–23,600 while crude headlines keep risk appetite selective. Nifty holds 23,274–23,218 and rotates within a broad 23,200–23,600 range. Bank Nifty stays between its 54,699 support and 55,054 resistance. This is a stock-selection session: let the first 30–60 minutes establish whether Monday’s breadth can persist.

🔴 Bear case • gap failure and oil shock

The reported Qeshm escalation lifts Brent, global technology weakness deepens and Nifty loses 23,274, then 23,218 and 23,129. A break of 23,000 would put the recovery at risk. Bank Nifty below 54,699 exposes 54,590 and then 54,412. A rising India VIX, weaker advance/decline ratio and renewed foreign selling would reinforce the downside case.

The GIFT indication is only an opening reference. Acceptance or rejection of 23,500, breadth after the first hour, Bank Nifty confirmation and the direction of crude are more useful than the pre-open gap alone.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Keep the higher-timeframe sequence

Start with monthly structure, refine on weekly and daily charts, and use the 2-hour chart for execution. Monday’s strong candle improves the short-term picture, but it does not erase the larger trend or remove the need for a predefined invalidation level.

2 • Do not chase the opening gap

Let the first 15–30 minutes establish the opening range. Prefer a pullback that holds 23,500 after a breakout, a clean retest with volume, or a 2-hour close above resistance. A gap that immediately fills is a warning, not an automatic dip-buying opportunity.

3 • Demand leadership beyond Reliance

Prioritise stocks whose sector is outperforming, whose price and volume confirm accumulation, and whose relative strength versus Nifty is improving. Monday’s rally had broad participation, but Reliance contributed an unusually large share of the benchmark move; look for confirmation from other leaders.

4 • Respect event and geopolitical risk

Check Q2 results, the IDBI Bank strike, IPO listings, corporate actions and the F&O ban before entering. Size positions for overnight gaps, define invalidation before entry and avoid holding oversized exposure through oil or election headlines.

5 • One high-quality setup beats a crowded watchlist

For a 3–90 day swing, focus on the strongest relative-strength candidate rather than spreading risk across correlated names. Require a clear entry trigger, stop, reward-to-risk and a catalyst that can sustain beyond one session.

6 • Reassess after the close

Record whether 23,500 held, whether volume confirmed, how the stock behaved against its sector and whether the closing price remained near the day’s high. Update the plan only after the daily candle is complete.

Final Market View

Positive opening bias — but the oil headline makes gap discipline essential.

Monday’s 1.68% Nifty advance, strong breadth, FII net buying and GIFT Nifty near 23,531 give the bulls a constructive starting point. The market has reclaimed 23,400 and momentum has improved. But the Nasdaq’s decline, Asia’s mixed tone and the reported US strike on Iran’s Qeshm Island have brought geopolitical oil risk straight back into focus. Nifty must sustain 23,500–23,507 and then clear 23,597, while Bank Nifty needs to reclaim 55,164 to confirm the move. If crude stabilises near $105 and breadth holds, selective upside remains possible; if oil jumps and the opening gap fails, protect capital rather than defend a bullish view.

Bias: cautiously positive • wait for confirmation

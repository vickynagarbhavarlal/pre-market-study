TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Friday's bounce is not yet a trend reversal; 24,000 is the line that matters.

Nifty recovered 0.35% on Friday, but it remains below major moving averages and below the 24,350–24,400 structural hurdle. With GIFT Nifty weaker and global risk sentiment deteriorating, the first test is whether 24,100–24,075 holds. The deeper support zone is 24,000–23,800. A reclaim of 24,200 can stabilise the tape; a sustained move above 24,300–24,400 would be the stronger bullish confirmation.

05

Stock-Specific News Radar

Today's strongest company-level triggers, separated by likely market read-through.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

HDFC Bank

MD & CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan will retire on 26 October 2026 after deciding not to seek reappointment. The board is fast-tracking the successor process. Reuters also reports the bank is weighing Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha and an external candidate. This is a major index-weight and sentiment risk.

🟢 BULLISH

Hero MotoCorp / Ather Energy

Hero MotoCorp completed the purchase of about 1.19 crore Ather shares from the Government of Singapore for ₹1,758 Cr, lifting its stake to about 32.8%. The deal increases strategic exposure to the EV two-wheeler market.

🟢 BULLISH

Sterlite Technologies

STL signed a long-term contract worth $288 million with a leading hyperscaler. The order supports the company's data-centre connectivity and optical-fibre growth narrative.

🟢 BULLISH

Avantel

Avantel received a ₹117.88 Cr DRDO contract for development, installation and commissioning of a Ground Segment Hub for voice and data communication. The order is material relative to the company's scale.

🟢 BULLISH

Max Estates

Max Estates agreed to acquire promoter-owned land-holding companies owning about 84.71 acres in West Delhi through a share swap. The parcel is estimated to unlock ₹10,000–12,000 Cr of gross development value.

🟢 BULLISH

NBCC

NBCC signed an MoU with NHPC for infrastructure projects across India, including residential, commercial and office infrastructure, at a 5.5% project-management consultancy fee.

🟢 BULLISH

Ashoka Buildcon

Ashoka Buildcon received a ₹602 Cr order from RVNL. The contract adds visibility to the order book and is a meaningful near-term project-flow trigger.

🟢 BULLISH

Reliance Industries

Jio Platforms received a SEBI observation letter on the DRHP filed for its proposed IPO. The development advances the process toward the planned public offering and keeps Reliance in focus.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Aurobindo Pharma

US FDA completed an inspection of Apitoria Pharma's Unit-VI/API facility and issued three procedural observations in Form 483. The observations are procedural, but regulatory headlines can create short-term volatility.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

SpiceJet

SpiceJet temporarily halted Boeing 737 MAX operations amid leasing disputes. The operational disruption is a direct company-specific risk and should remain on the watchlist.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

R R Kabel / JSW Cement

R R Kabel received a show-cause-cum-demand notice from the Surat tax department, while JSW Cement received a similar notice from Jajpur. These are legal/tax overhangs; financial impact should not be assumed without further disclosure.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

ICICI Bank / SBI

ICICI Bank completed a $1 billion senior-notes issuance under its GMTN programme. SBI's arm plans to dilute up to 1% in the NSE IPO. Both are important capital-markets developments but need to be separated from immediate earnings triggers.

Macro & Event Watch

India Q1 FY27 GDP

GDP data is due around 4:00 PM today. Reuters polling points to about 7.1% growth versus 7.8% in the prior quarter. A meaningful upside/downside surprise can move bonds, banks and the index late in the session.

July Fiscal Deficit

July fiscal-deficit data is scheduled for 3:30 PM. The number matters for bond yields and the fiscal trajectory, especially with the 10-year benchmark already near 6.91%.

MSCI changes take effect after today's close. Laurus Labs, Lenskart, Adani Energy Solutions and Groww enter the Standard Index, while Balkrishna Industries, SBI Cards and Astral exit. Closing-auction flows may be unusually large.

Scenario Map

BULLISH RELIEF

24,220 → 24,300

If Nifty absorbs the opening weakness and reclaims 24,200–24,220, a relief move toward 24,300 becomes possible. Above 24,400 is the stronger confirmation.

BASE CASE

24,075–24,250

Volatile range trade is the cleanest base case while traders wait for GDP/fiscal data and the MSCI closing-auction flows. Friday's rebound alone is not enough to turn the trend bullish.

BEARISH BREAK

Below 24,000

A sustained break of 24,000 would invalidate the immediate support thesis and put 23,800 into play. Higher oil and fresh FII selling would reinforce the downside case.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 31 AUG 2026

CAUTIOUS — Friday's rebound faces a tougher global morning.

The market has two competing forces: strong domestic institutional support and improving selective corporate news on one side, versus renewed Middle-East risk, higher oil, higher US-rate expectations and a ₹5,040 Cr FII outflow on the other. Do not extrapolate Friday's IT-led bounce. The clean technical decision is 24,000 on the downside and 24,200–24,400 on the upside. Today's GDP, fiscal data and MSCI closing flows can create a second volatility window late in the session.

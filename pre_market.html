FINAL MARKET VIEW

Neutral-to-cautious setup until Nifty proves it can reclaim 23,500–23,600.

The market lost Tuesday's session despite easing crude and supportive Asian cues. FII selling of ₹3,810 Cr was substantial, although DII buying of ₹4,120 Cr provided a strong counterweight. For Wednesday, lower oil is the main macro positive, while the failed move into 23,500–23,600 and the bearish daily structure remain the technical concern. Focus on whether 23,290–23,165 holds first; a sustained reclaim of 23,500–23,600 would materially change the setup.

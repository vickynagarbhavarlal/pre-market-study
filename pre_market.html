10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Global strength does not guarantee a strong Indian open when GIFT Nifty is lower and crude remains elevated. Let the first 30–60 minutes show whether price accepts above the pivot or rejects resistance. Treat Jio listing volatility and Q2 headlines as event risk, not as a substitute for a chart setup.

Use higher timeframes

Start monthly, then weekly, then daily charts; use the 2-hour chart as the lowest timeframe for refinement. Prioritise relative strength versus Nifty, clean bases or breakout-retests, supportive volume and a clear invalidation level. Avoid forcing trades in a flat index.

Define risk first

Set stop-loss and position size before entry. If volatility widens the stop distance, reduce size rather than loosening the stop. Check earnings dates, liquidity and overnight-gap risk; do not buy solely because an IPO GMP is high or a headline sounds bullish.

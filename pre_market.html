TCS

RBL Bank

CareEdge assigned BBB+/Stable to RBL Bank's USD 1 billion EMTN programme and issuer. The rating is supportive of funding-market access, but it does not remove the broader banking-sector pressure visible in Tuesday's session.

🟢 BULLISH

Eureka Forbes

CRISIL upgraded Eureka Forbes' corporate credit rating to AA/Stable from AA-/Positive. The improvement is a constructive credit-quality signal, though the stock still trades within a risk-off market backdrop.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Shadowfax Technologies

TPG-backed Newquest sold 80 lakh shares at ₹250.03 while Oxbow Master Fund bought 47.85 lakh shares at ₹250. The divergent block activity matters for ownership and supply dynamics.

IndiGo has started international operations from Northeast India, according to the company statement. The route expansion is strategically positive, but aviation demand and fuel costs remain important variables.

MosChip Technologies

The company clarified that the Supreme Court's September 1 order modified the status-quo position in the Vayavya Labs acquisition matter and reiterated it is not a party to the underlying dispute. Management says it expects no financial or operational impact.

Scenario Map

Bull case

23,800 → 24,000

A sharp reclaim of 23,750–23,800 followed by sustained trade above it would signal that buyers are absorbing the oil shock. 24,000 remains the larger confirmation hurdle.

Base case

23,600–23,800

Volatile consolidation around the swing-low is the more credible base case if Brent stabilises below $100 and domestic institutions absorb supply.

Bear case

23,600 → 23,300

A decisive break of 23,600/23,635 would damage the support structure and open the way toward 23,300. Do not average aggressively into that breakdown.

09

Swing-Trader Framework

Long setup

Only after reclaim

Prefer longs only if Nifty reclaims 23,800 and holds it with breadth. A higher-high/higher-low sequence matters more than a single gap-up.

Short setup

Breakdown below 23,600

If 23,600 breaks decisively, favour relative-strength shorts with defined stops rather than trying to catch the falling index.

Risk control

Smaller size

Crude near $100 + weak index structure + geopolitical headlines is a poor environment for oversized swing positions. Keep risk per trade tight.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Bearish until proven otherwise. 23,600 is today's battlefield.

GIFT Nifty is pointing to a weak opening, while Brent near $100, a softer rupee and higher U.S. yields reinforce the risk-off setup. The market can bounce from the 23,600 area, but a bounce is not a reversal. The first technical repair is 23,750–23,800; the bigger confirmation remains 24,000. Below 23,600, step aside rather than trying to predict the bottom.

TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

24,000 is the market's first real defence; 24,200–24,300 is the first recovery test.

Thursday's selloff pushed Nifty below 24,100 and below its key moving averages. The 24,000–23,950 area is the immediate support cluster, while 24,200–24,300 is the first supply zone on any rebound. A decisive break below 23,950 would weaken the structure further toward 23,800; a sustained reclaim of 24,200–24,230 would improve the near-term tone.

Stock-Specific News Radar

Only material company-specific triggers are included; generic sector commentary is kept separate.

🟢 BULLISH

Tejas Networks

TCS has issued a ₹1,537 Cr Letter of Intent to Tejas Networks for RAN equipment supply. The size of the order makes this a meaningful order-book/capacity-utilisation trigger.

🟢 BULLISH

Great Eastern Shipping

Board approved a ₹900 Cr buyback at up to ₹1,530/share, up to 58.8 lakh shares or 4.12% of equity. The size and premium mechanics make the corporate action important for near-term sentiment.

🟢 BULLISH

FDC

Delhi High Court stayed the FSSAI notice on electrolyte claims and allowed eight months to clear existing inventory/update labels. The company says there is no material operating or financial impact.

🟢 BULLISH

Hero MotoCorp

Hero will acquire an additional stake in Ather Energy for ₹1,758 Cr, taking its holding to 32.8% from 29.88%. This increases strategic exposure to the electric two-wheeler ecosystem.

🟢 BULLISH

Wipro

Wipro expanded its partnership with Google Cloud around Gemini Enterprise and agentic AI adoption. The trigger is strategically positive for its AI/cloud positioning, though revenue impact is not immediate.

🟢 BULLISH

NBCC / RailTel

NBCC received work orders worth ₹134 Cr from multiple government bodies, while RailTel received a ₹38 Cr order from Cotton Corporation of India.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

HDFC Bank

HDFC Bank fell 2.2% on Thursday to a 2.5-year low amid reports around a US class-action lawsuit and uncertainty around CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan's tenure renewal. This remains a major index-weight risk.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

RPP Infra Projects

A ₹206 Cr Tamil Nadu work order was terminated. The cancellation directly affects the order-book narrative and is a negative company-specific trigger.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Allcargo Global Logistics

July LCL volumes declined 6% YoY to 728 thousand cubic metres. The volume trend is the immediate negative operating signal.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Hindustan Copper

The government/promoter disposed of 5.80 Cr shares, while the board will consider QIP and additional fundraising later. Supply overhang and capital-raising expectations can keep the stock volatile.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Adani Enterprises

AdaniConneX acquired 100% of Chandenvalle Infra Park from Adani Power for ₹536 Cr. Strategically relevant for data-centre infrastructure, but the immediate market read-through depends on valuation and integration.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Cipla / ICICI Bank

Cipla's JV Aspergen acquired 100% of Aspergen Inc. ICICI Bank completed a USD 1 Bn senior-notes issuance under its GMTN programme. Both are material corporate developments but not necessarily immediate earnings movers.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 28 AUG 2026

CAUTIOUS — watch 24,000 first, then 24,200.

The overnight US rally is real, but India's own technical structure remains weak. The correct way to read today's session is not “Nvidia up = India up”; it is whether Nifty can defend 24,000 and then reclaim 24,200–24,300. Strong DII buying on 27 Aug is a cushion, while HDFC Bank weakness and foreign selling remain risks. A relief bounce is possible, but confirmation matters more than the opening print.

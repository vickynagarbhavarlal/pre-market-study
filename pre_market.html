FINAL MARKET VIEW

High-volatility session: 23,000 is the key support, while 23,200–23,300 is the first recovery hurdle.

Thursday's 1.64% Nifty decline materially changed the short-term structure. The index broke below the recent consolidation floor as Brent surged, US Treasury yields reached multi-year highs and FII selling intensified. Friday's mildly positive GIFT Nifty and firmer Asian cues offer some opening relief, but they do not by themselves reverse the technical breakdown. The first objective is to see whether 23,000 holds and whether any rebound can reclaim 23,200–23,300.

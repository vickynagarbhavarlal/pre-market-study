01

Overnight Global Cues

Risk appetite improved after Wall Street's late-session recovery.

🇺🇸 US markets • positive close

Wednesday's session ended higher: Dow Jones gained 0.85%, S&P 500 rose 0.50% and Nasdaq advanced 1.48%. The rebound followed a volatile technology-led session earlier in the week. US cash markets will remain closed today for Thanksgiving, which may reduce global participation and liquidity.

🌏 Asia • broadly firmer

Asian equities opened higher, with the MSCI Asia-Pacific index up around 0.4% in early trade. Japanese and South Korean shares led the early improvement, while investors continued to assess the durability of the AI-led rally and demand for safer assets.

📈 GIFT Nifty • gap-up indication

GIFT Nifty was around 26,101 at 06:37 IST, indicating a premium of roughly 164 points over the previous Nifty futures reference. This points to a higher start, but the cash-market opening may differ as the futures basis and overseas prices move.

🛢️ Crude oil

Brent was near $90.64 a barrel in early indications. Oil remains a key variable for India's import bill, inflation expectations and downstream margins. A sustained retreat would be supportive for the rupee and oil-sensitive sectors; a renewed rise could reverse that benefit.

💱 Currency

USD/INR was indicated around ₹92.22. The rupee's recent strengthening has helped sentiment, but watch the opening move and any reversal in the dollar. A weaker rupee can affect import-heavy businesses and foreign portfolio flows.

📊 Global yields

The US 10-year Treasury yield was around 4.85%. India's 10-year government bond yield was last indicated near 7.16%. With US markets shut for Thanksgiving, fresh global price discovery may be thinner through the session.

Sector Radar

🟢 FMCG • strongest

Nifty FMCG gained 3.79%, led by heavyweight consumer names. HUL rose 6.08% and Tata Consumer Products advanced 4.66%. Softer crude and input-cost expectations may support sentiment, but watch whether the sharp one-day move attracts profit booking.

🟢 Pharma & Healthcare

Nifty Pharma gained around 2.38% and Healthcare advanced 2.13%. The sector participated in the recovery, with stock-specific regulatory approvals and earnings updates adding catalysts. Prefer names holding above VWAP and showing relative strength.

🟢 Private Banks

Private Bank gained around 2.21%, helping Bank Nifty rebound. The sector needs follow-through buying and a sustained move above key banking resistance levels to confirm that the rebound is more than short covering.

🔴 Media • weakest

Nifty Media declined around 4.4%, making it the weakest major sector despite the broader market rally. Avoid assuming that index strength will lift every constituent; demand a fresh base and improving relative performance.

🔴 Metals

Nifty Metal fell around 2.0%. Tata Steel, Hindalco and other metal names remained under pressure. Monitor global metal prices, Chinese demand signals and stock-specific transaction or funding concerns before looking for a reversal.

🟠 Broader market

Midcap and smallcap indices lagged the headline benchmarks. The rally was therefore led more by large, liquid names. Swing traders should prioritise stocks with independent strength rather than buying indiscriminately across the broader market.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Don't chase the gap

GIFT Nifty indicates a positive start, but the Nifty is approaching the 26,000 resistance area. Let the first 15–30 minutes establish direction. A gap that loses its opening range low is a warning, not an invitation to average.

2 • Use the pivot as the balance line

Nifty 25,797 is the central reference, with 25,974 as R1 and 25,642 as S1. Trade only when price action, VWAP and breadth support the same direction; levels alone are not entry signals.

3 • Demand banking confirmation

Bank Nifty remains below its Friday close near 64,635. A sustained move above 64,000 and then 64,635 would improve the recovery quality. Weak banks can limit Nifty follow-through.

4 • Watch the lagging small caps

Smallcap indices did not participate meaningfully in Wednesday's rally. Avoid broad buying based only on benchmark strength; focus on liquid stocks with strong relative performance and clean risk levels.

5 • Respect the holiday liquidity effect

US cash markets are closed for Thanksgiving. Thin global participation can produce less reliable overnight signals and sharper moves around local flows. Avoid oversized positions based on a single futures snapshot.

6 • Define risk before entry

India VIX remains near 17.6. Adjust position size to stop distance, avoid averaging losing trades without a valid setup and book partial profits near planned resistance rather than converting a trade into an investment.

Final Market View

The recovery has improved; 26,000 is the next test.

Wednesday's 1.85% Nifty rebound, positive institutional flows and a firmer global backdrop support a positive opening bias. However, the rally was led by large caps while small caps slipped, and Nifty closed below its intraday high near 25,952. The GIFT indication around 26,101 puts the market close to a major psychological and technical resistance zone. A sustained move above 25,974–26,000, supported by Bank Nifty above 64,000 and improving breadth, can extend the recovery towards 26,128. If the gap fades and Nifty loses 25,797, the 25,642–25,466 support band comes into focus. The preferred approach is constructive but selective: let the opening settle, demand confirmation and avoid chasing an extended first move.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase the gap

GIFT Nifty is an indication, not a guarantee. Let the first 30–60 minutes show whether 23,000 holds and breadth confirms.

Use higher timeframes first

Start with monthly and weekly charts, then daily structure. One strong session does not repair a weak medium-term trend; define invalidation and size to stop distance.

Respect event risk

Q2 results, high crude and foreign outflows can trigger reversals. Avoid oversized positions, averaging down into broken setups or treating IPO GMP as a reliable forecast.

Final Market View

Positive opening cue — confirmation required

GIFT Nifty around 23,082–23,103 points to a positive start after Nifty gained 1.66% to 22,997.35 on Thursday. Lower US yields, stronger Asian equities and SEBI’s expiry-settlement reversal are supportive. However, Brent near $105–106 and FII net selling of ₹5,285 crore remain risks. A sustained move above 23,000–23,057 may extend toward 23,156 and 23,300–23,350; failure to hold 22,850 and then 22,700–22,600 would weaken the recovery. Prefer selective, risk-defined setups and avoid chasing the first spike.

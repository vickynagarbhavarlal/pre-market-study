TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Respect the rebound — but do not confuse a gap-up with a reversal.

Nifty closed at a five-month low of 23,118.60 after a 1.19% fall. Tuesday's breadth was weak and FII selling was heavy. GIFT Nifty indicates a higher start, but Brent near $108, USD/INR near ₹96 and U.S. yields above 5% keep the macro backdrop hostile. A sustained move above 23,300 can extend toward 23,500–23,650; rejection there keeps the broader downtrend intact. Below 23,000, downside risk reopens toward 22,700–22,800.

Sector Radar

🟢 IT — Relative strength

IT was the standout pocket on Tuesday as valuations and AI-related sentiment supported buying. However, U.S. yields above 5% remain a risk to the sector's valuation multiple.

🔴 Auto — Oil sensitive

Autos were among Tuesday's weaker groups. Higher crude can raise operating costs and compress demand, while the weak rupee adds to some input pressures.

🔴 Defence / broader midcaps

Broader and defence-linked counters faced aggressive selling. Do not treat an index rebound as proof that high-beta stocks have bottomed.

🟠 Banks

Bank Nifty remains below important moving-average and supply zones. Prefer relative-strength leaders rather than buying the entire financial basket blindly.

🟠 FMCG

Defensive consumption can attract relative interest if macro volatility persists, but the setup still needs price confirmation after the broad market sell-off.

🟠 Energy

Upstream energy can benefit from high crude, while downstream users face margin pressure. Stock selection matters more than the sector label.

Scenario Map

🟢 BULL CASE

23,300 breaks and holds

If the gap-up survives the first hour and Nifty sustains above 23,300, a relief move toward 23,500–23,650 becomes viable. Banks and large caps should confirm.

🟠 RANGE CASE

23,000–23,300 churn

Likely if buyers defend 23,000 but macro risk prevents a breakout. Avoid overtrading; wait for a clean range resolution.

🔴 BEAR CASE

23,000 fails

A sustained break below 23,000 would negate the rebound setup and expose 22,700–22,800. High-beta and weak relative-strength names should be avoided.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do

Wait for structure

Use the daily chart as the decision timeframe. A single green session after a sharp fall is not enough; look for higher lows, reclaim of resistance and improving volume.

Avoid

Chasing the gap

Do not buy a stock merely because the market opens higher. If the index is below major supply, stock selection should favour relative strength and clean risk-reward.

Risk

Reduce position size

With crude near $108, U.S. yields above 5% and the Fed decision pending, event risk is high. Keep invalidation levels defined before entry.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Rebound bias for the open. Bearish structure until proven otherwise.

The market is oversold enough for a technical bounce, and GIFT Nifty supports a positive start. But the combination of heavy FII selling, high crude, a weak rupee and elevated U.S. yields means the burden of proof remains on the bulls. For swing trades, the better signal is not the opening gap; it is whether price can build above 23,300 and then reclaim 23,500 with breadth.

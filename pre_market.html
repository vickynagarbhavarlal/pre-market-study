01

Global Overnight Cues

US futures tilt positive

US index futures were higher in early Asian trade, led by Nasdaq futures, as investors positioned for the earnings season. Strong yields and uncertainty around AI-led returns limit confidence in a broad risk-on move.

Asia mixed; Japan closed

Asian equities were mixed to subdued, with South Korea weaker and Japan closed for a holiday. Regional breadth is not strong enough to validate an aggressive opening rally.

Oil and geopolitics remain central

Brent was reported around $103.50/bbl in early trade. Middle East shipping and energy-infrastructure risks are keeping oil elevated despite a modest pullback. This is a key risk for India's import bill, inflation and the rupee.

Indian Market Setup

Friday’s recovery snapped a long losing streak, but the larger trend remains fragile until Nifty reclaims higher resistance with sustained participation.

Nifty 50

Market breadth and trend

Friday’s strong move followed a sharp selloff. A single rebound session does not repair the larger structure. Look for broad participation, follow-through buying and support holding through the first hour before increasing exposure.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Let the first 30–60 minutes establish the range. Track breadth, price acceptance around 22,500 and whether the opening gap holds. A positive pre-open indication is not an entry signal.

Use higher timeframes

Start from monthly and weekly context, then confirm on the daily chart. Trade only a defined setup with a clear invalidation level, position size tied to stop distance and acceptable reward/risk.

Preserve capital

Persistent FPI selling, high oil and a fragile larger trend argue for selectivity. Do not average down into a broken structure; a watchlist is not an obligation to trade.

Final Market View

Positive open possible; confirmation still required

Friday’s 1.30% Nifty rebound and early GIFT Nifty indication support a firmer start. The key near-term test is 22,800, with 23,000 above; on the downside, 22,500 and then 22,180 are important references. FPI selling continues despite domestic buying, while crude near $103.50, a weak rupee and elevated US yields keep macro risk high. Prefer selective, risk-defined swing setups and wait for price/breadth confirmation rather than chasing the gap.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not chase the ninth advance

A nine-session regular-market winning streak can continue, but the reward-to-risk of late entries deteriorates when price is extended. Wait for a controlled pullback, a fresh base or a decisive breakout-retest.

2 • Let breadth validate the index

Wednesday's NSE advance/decline ratio of 2.08 was constructive. A further advance with improving breadth is stronger evidence than an index lift driven only by a few banks or large caps.

3 • Separate earnings from price reaction

Tata Power, Hindustan Copper, Cupid, GRSE and MIDHANI reported strong growth; Triveni Turbine was weak and Aurobindo has an FDA overhang. Compare guidance, margins and valuation with the market's reaction.

4 • Respect rates and currency risk

India's 10-year yield rose to 7.217%; the dollar index is near a one-month high. These are relevant counterweights to the domestic earnings and FII-flow tailwind, especially for rate-sensitive names.

5 • Use index levels for invalidation

Nifty's 25,258 pivot and 25,092 S1 frame the first balance zone; 25,421 R1 is the upside pivot reference. Bank Nifty's 60,857 pivot and 60,323 S1 provide equivalent levels. Define the stop before entry.

6 • Reduce event-gap exposure

Twenty-two companies are scheduled to report today. Avoid carrying oversized positions through results unless the gap risk is deliberately part of the trade plan. Do not average a failed breakout or widen a stop.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Let price confirm

Do not chase the first gap. Let the opening range settle and require price confirmation with sector participation.

Respect higher timeframes

Start monthly and weekly, then daily. Use the 2-hour chart to refine timing, not to override a damaged daily setup.

Define risk first

Prefer liquid stocks with verified catalysts and relative strength. Set invalidation first, size positions to stop distance and avoid averaging down into broken setups.

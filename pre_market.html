Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Respect the extended move

Nifty has rallied over 2,100 points in nine sessions. Do not buy only because the daily trend is strong; let support be tested and a tradable base form.

2 • Define invalidation

For bullish setups monitor 24,300–24,180 first and 23,960–24,296 as the wider breakout zone. A sustained close below the gap weakens the swing thesis.

3 • Relative strength

Prefer stocks outperforming Nifty and their sector, with volume confirmation and a verifiable earnings or business catalyst.

4 • Event-aware entries

Today’s results, ex-dates, F&O restrictions and Sunday Muhurat session can create gaps. Avoid oversized positions through binary events.

5 • Risk before reward

Size from stop distance and account risk. Do not average a failed breakout; predefine entry, invalidation, target and time stop.

6 • Review the close

Record whether Nifty held 24,300, Bank Nifty defended 57,250 and breadth stabilised. Reassess after the daily candle and official flows.

Risk & Execution Checklist

Position sizing

Use fixed rupee risk per trade. Volatility is elevated; reduce quantity rather than widening stops.

Market confirmation

GIFT Nifty is an overnight signal only. Wait for cash-market acceptance, breadth and sector confirmation.

Calendar and liquidity

Check results, ex-dates, F&O ban status and the altered expiry/holiday calendar. Avoid thin liquidity around events.

11

Final Market View

Strong domestic trend meets a severe global risk-off shock.

Thursday’s 2.40% Nifty rally, 3.33% Bank Nifty advance and exceptional FII net buying of ₹14,974.68 crore provide a domestic cushion. But Nasdaq −5.52%, Kospi around −8% and Nikkei more than −6% argue against complacency. GIFT Nifty at 24,425–24,465 signals a weaker start. First Nifty demand is 24,300–24,180; the wider breakout zone is 23,960–24,296. Bank Nifty support is 57,250–56,900. Recovery above 24,630/24,750 with breadth restores momentum; failure below 24,180 invites deeper profit-taking. Trade smaller, wait for confirmation and do not confuse one exceptional FII session with immunity from global volatility.

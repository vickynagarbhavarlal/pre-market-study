Oil relief can support the opening, but 22,700 remains the key downside line and 23,000 the main recovery threshold.

Nifty closed at 22,716.20 on Tuesday after another decline, while foreign investors sold ₹9,980.22 Cr. Brent has eased toward $103–104 and GIFT Nifty is around 22,834. The session also carries significant index-rebalance and new-listing activity. Watch whether the market can hold above 22,700 and convert 22,800–23,000 into support rather than assuming that a positive opening alone repairs the trend.

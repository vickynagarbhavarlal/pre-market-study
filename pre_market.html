TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Positive gap — but don't confuse it with a confirmed breakout.

Oil falling sharply and improving Hormuz headlines are genuine positives for India. Tuesday's Nifty recovery also came with FII buying. But the index is entering the 24,350–24,500 resistance band, including the 200-day EMA area. Sustained acceptance above 24,500 is the cleaner bullish confirmation; rejection can pull the index back toward 24,200–24,100.

Today's Scenario Map

🟢 BULLISH

Above 24,500

Sustained acceptance above 24,500 can open the path toward 24,650 and improve the September-series trend setup.

🟠 RANGE / REJECTION

24,200–24,500

A strong opening followed by rejection around 24,400–24,500 would favour two-way trade and profit-taking.

🔴 BEARISH

Below 24,100

Loss of the immediate support zone would weaken the recovery; below 24,000 the downside setup becomes materially stronger.

FOR SWING TRADERS

Let the market prove the gap.

The opening cue is strong, but the trade is not “buy because GIFT Nifty is up.” The cleaner setup is either a sustained breakout above 24,480–24,500 or a controlled pullback that holds 24,200–24,100 and turns back up. Avoid chasing the first spike if price immediately meets supply.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 26 AUGUST 2026

Positive setup — breakout confirmation above 24,500.

Falling oil, improving Hormuz headlines, positive US equities and fresh FII buying all support the opening. The market's job today is to prove that strength above the 24,350–24,500 resistance band. Above 24,500 → bullish continuation. 24,200–24,500 → range/absorption. Below 24,100 → caution. Below 24,000 → bearish reversal risk.

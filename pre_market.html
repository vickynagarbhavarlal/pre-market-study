🟢 Bull case • support holds

Nifty defends 23,576–23,600, reclaims 23,827–23,904 and breadth remains positive. A sustained break above 24,000 can open 24,100–24,200. Bank Nifty above 55,834–56,049 confirms financial participation.

🟠 Base case • consolidation

Weak GIFT and mixed global cues prompt a cautious open, but Nifty holds 23,500–23,600 and rotates below 24,000. Bank Nifty holds 55,135–55,834. Stock selection matters more than chasing index momentum.

🔴 Bear case • support failure

Rising yields, a stronger dollar or oil reversal trigger profit-taking. Nifty loses 23,499 then 23,373; Bank Nifty slips below 54,920. Weak breadth and reversal in FII flows reinforce the downside case.

09

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Higher-timeframe sequence

Start monthly, then weekly and daily; use 2-hour charts for execution. Four bullish sessions improve the short-term structure but do not erase 23,900–24,200 resistance.

2 • Do not chase a weak open

Wait 15–30 minutes. Prefer a reclaim and successful retest of 23,600, or a decisive close above 23,904 with volume. A gap-down that cannot recover 23,600 is a warning.

3 • Demand sector confirmation

Choose stocks outperforming Nifty and their sector, with volume/delivery confirmation and a clear earnings or business catalyst.

4 • Control event risk

Check results, corporate actions, IPO listing volatility and F&O ban status. Size for overnight gaps and define invalidation before entry.

5 • One quality setup

For a 3–90 day swing, favour one or a few uncorrelated setups with a defined trigger, stop, target and reward-to-risk.

6 • Review after close

Record whether Nifty held 23,600, Bank Nifty confirmed and breadth broadened. Change the plan only after the daily candle completes.

10

Risk & Execution Checklist

Position sizing

Set quantity from entry-to-invalidation distance. Do not widen stops because the market opens against the trade; reduce size when volatility rises.

Event filter

Check earnings, corporate filings, IPO listings and derivative restrictions before carrying positions overnight.

Confirmation over prediction

Do not infer a breakout from GIFT or a single global index. Wait for cash-market acceptance, volume and sector participation.

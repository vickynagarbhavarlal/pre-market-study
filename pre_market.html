Overnight Global Cues

US cash markets were shut for Thanksgiving; Asian markets are mostly higher, while crude remains a concern.

🇺🇸 US markets • holiday session

US cash equity markets were closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving. Trading resumes today with a shortened session, so overnight cash-market price discovery is limited. US index futures were modestly higher in early indications, with Dow futures around +0.55% and Nasdaq futures around +0.64% at approximately 06:38 IST.

🌏 Asia • positive but measured

Asian markets were mostly higher: South Korea's Kospi gained around 0.77%, Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 0.47% and Australia's S&P/ASX 200 advanced 0.40%. The improvement supports sentiment, but it is not a strong enough signal to ignore the domestic resistance zone or crude-price pressure.

📈 GIFT Nifty • mildly lower start

GIFT Nifty was around 25,996.50, down approximately 35.50 points or 0.14% in the early indication. This suggests a flat-to-slightly-negative opening after two sessions of gains. The futures indication can change before the cash open and should not be treated as a precise opening forecast.

🛢️ Crude oil • elevated risk

Brent was near $96.47–$96.19 a barrel in early reports, close to its November high, while WTI was around $85.41. Supply concerns linked to disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz and regional energy infrastructure have lifted risk premiums. Higher crude is a headwind for India's import bill, inflation and fuel-sensitive sectors.

💱 Currency

The rupee closed around ₹92.23 per US dollar on Thursday, weakening by approximately 22 paise. A softer rupee alongside elevated crude can increase imported inflation pressure. Watch the opening exchange-rate indication and whether dollar demand persists.

📊 Global yields

The US 10-year Treasury yield was last indicated near 4.85%; Thursday's US bond trading was affected by the Thanksgiving holiday. India's 10-year government bond yield remains around the 7.1% area in the latest available reference. Treat these as indicative levels, not live quotes.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Treat 26,000 as a decision zone

Nifty has failed to close above 26,000 despite two sessions of gains. Wait for acceptance above the level, not a brief intraday spike. A failed breakout can quickly reverse towards the pivot.

2 • Demand banking confirmation

Bank Nifty closed lower on Thursday. A Nifty breakout without participation from large banks can be narrow and fragile. Track HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI and Axis Bank for relative strength and VWAP behaviour.

3 • IT leadership is extended

IT gained 3.66% in one session. Avoid buying a stock simply because its sector was the strongest. Prefer pullbacks that hold support or fresh consolidations with volume confirmation.

4 • Respect the crude-price risk

Brent near $96 adds macro uncertainty for India. Oil-sensitive companies can react differently depending on pricing power and hedging. Avoid treating a crude move as uniformly positive or negative across every sector.

5 • Keep listing and block-deal supply in view

Mahanadi Coalfields lists today and IGI has a proposed promoter block sale. Listing volatility and institutional supply can distort intraday price action. Keep event trades separate from ordinary swing positions.

6 • Control exposure

VIX has eased but remains a risk gauge, not a guarantee of calm trading. Size positions according to stop distance, avoid averaging losing trades and define invalidation before entry.

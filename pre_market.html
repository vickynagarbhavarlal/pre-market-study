TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Do not confuse a positive GIFT Nifty with a repaired market. 23,500–23,600 must be reclaimed and held.

Friday's close at 23,398 extended the fifth straight weekly decline. The Tuesday setup is complicated: GIFT Nifty points higher, but Brent is around $107, the U.S. 10-year yield has breached 5%, the rupee closed near ₹95.55 and Asian equities are subdued. If Nifty holds above 23,500 with breadth, a move toward 23,650–23,800 is possible. Failure can drag the index back toward 23,300 and 23,000–23,100.

Swing-Trader Framework

Long setup

Reclaim first

Do not chase the gap. Prefer longs only after 23,500–23,600 is reclaimed and held with breadth and sector participation.

Short setup

Below 23,300

A clean breakdown below 23,300 with weak breadth favours relative-strength shorts. Do not average into a breakdown.

Risk control

Smaller size

Oil above $100, a 5% U.S. 10-year and Fed-event risk create gap risk. Oversized overnight positions are unjustified.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Positive opening signal, but the trend is still bearish until 23,600 is reclaimed.

Today's setup is a classic trap zone: GIFT Nifty is positive while crude, yields and geopolitics are negative. The market can bounce because it is oversold, but a bounce is not a reversal. Treat 23,500–23,600 as the decision band. Above it, 23,750–23,800 becomes possible; below 23,300, the market can revisit 23,000–23,100.

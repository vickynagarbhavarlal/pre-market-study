Global Overnight Cues

🔴 Wall Street • broad weakness

US equities ended Tuesday lower. Dow Jones fell 1.64% to 50,759.42, S&P 500 declined 1.03% to 7,340.53 and Nasdaq slipped 0.74% to 24,328.38. Technology weakness and elevated bond yields kept risk appetite under pressure.

🟢 Nvidia • strong earnings outlook

Nvidia reported Q3 FY27 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% year-on-year, with data-centre revenue of $107.6 billion. Adjusted EPS was $2.68 and Q4 revenue guidance was $130 billion. Shares gained about 4% in extended trading, supporting the semiconductor theme.

🟠 Asia • mixed, chip-led pockets stronger

At around 07:17 IST, 3 of 7 tracked Asia-Pacific markets were higher. Nikkei 225 was up 0.21%, Kospi +1.75% and Taiex +0.32%; ASX 200 was down 0.29%, Straits Times -0.80%, Hang Seng -0.34% and Shanghai Composite -0.50%. The regional picture is mixed rather than a uniform risk-on move.

🟢 US index futures • rebound attempt

Dow futures were up about 0.16% and Nasdaq futures about 0.97% in the morning snapshot, following Tuesday's cash-market losses. Nvidia's results are helping technology sentiment, but futures gains still need confirmation through the US cash session.

🔴 GIFT Nifty • softer opening signal

GIFT Nifty traded near 25,258.5 at approximately 07:17 IST, down 0.36% on the quote. This points to a cautious-to-negative start against Tuesday's Nifty close of 25,387.40. The futures basis and quote can change before the 09:15 cash open.

🟠 Gold • defensive demand remains relevant

Spot gold was around $3,855 an ounce in the early report, close to a 14-month low. A firm dollar and high yields remain headwinds, even as geopolitical risk keeps a degree of defensive demand in the market.

02

Indian Market Setup

🟢 Benchmarks • second session higher

Nifty 50 gained 96.30 points (+0.38%) to 25,387.40. Sensex rose 427.40 points (+0.52%) to 82,532.19. Bank Nifty advanced 697.35 points (+1.13%) to 62,273.25, after touching 62,442.20 — a fresh 52-week high.

🟠 Breadth • nearly balanced

The reported NSE cash-market tally was 1,815 advances, 1,813 declines and 120 unchanged. Midcap 100 rose 0.25%, while Smallcap 100 slipped 0.22%. The headline rally therefore had better support in large banks than in the broader market.

🟢 India VIX • eased

India VIX closed near 15.52, down about 1.02%. Lower implied volatility is supportive, but the level does not remove overnight gap risk or the possibility of sharp stock-specific moves.

🟠 Nifty • 25,500 remains the hurdle

Tuesday's open was 25,277.30, high 25,501.35, low 25,267.30 and close 25,387.40. Classic pivot: 25,385.35. Support: S1 25,269.35 and S2 25,151.30. Resistance: R1 25,503.40 and R2 25,619.40. A sustained move above 25,500 is needed to extend the breakout.

🟢 Bank Nifty • strong but stretched

Tuesday's open was 61,559.95, high 62,442.20, low 61,488.00 and close 62,273.25. Pivot: 62,067.82. Support: 61,693.43 and 61,113.62. Resistance: 62,647.63 and 63,022.02. Daily RSI near 79 indicates strong momentum but also a stretched short-term reading.

🟠 Opening discipline

A lower GIFT indication may test Tuesday's gains. Watch whether Nifty can reclaim 25,385 after the open and whether Bank Nifty holds above 62,068. The first 15–30 minutes should establish whether selling is absorbed; avoid treating the opening print as a confirmed reversal.

Sector Radar

🟢 PSU Banks • strongest

Nifty PSU Bank gained 1.95%, leading the sector pack. Several public-sector lenders participated, with PNB among the stronger constituents.

🟢 Realty and private banks

Realty advanced 1.94% and Private Bank gained 0.89%. Financial Services rose 0.64%. Banking leadership remains the main support for the headline indices.

🟢 Auto, metals and oil & gas

Auto rose 0.72%, Metal 0.58% and Oil & Gas 0.44%. These cyclical groups participated, though the sustainability of the move needs another session of confirmation.

🔴 IT • weakest major sector

Nifty IT declined 0.90%, with HCL Technologies and Infosys among the notable drags. Nvidia's results may improve sentiment for semiconductor-linked names, but Indian IT services have different demand drivers.

🔴 FMCG, consumer durables and energy

FMCG slipped 0.20%, Consumer Durables 0.24% and Energy 0.27%. Defensive participation was weak despite the benchmark advance.

🟠 Broader market • selective

Sixteen of 23 tracked sectoral indices advanced, but smallcaps ended lower and overall cash-market breadth was nearly even. Avoid assuming the benchmark strength has spread uniformly.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🟢 Ashoka Buildcon • positive order pipeline

Declared L1 for an NHAI EPC project quoted at ₹1,465 crore excluding GST, covering the Jujuwa–Gandeva section of the Vadodara–Mumbai Expressway. Execution is stated at 18 months. This is a positive order-pipeline catalyst, but L1 status is not the same as a final letter of award.

🟢 Advait Energy Transitions • GETCO order

Secured a ₹130.49 crore order from Gujarat Energy Transmission Corporation for reconductoring two 220 kV lines in Bharuch, with a 15-month execution period. September-quarter net profit rose 88.5% year-on-year to ₹19.18 crore. Execution and working-capital conversion remain key checks.

🟠 Prestige Estates • commercial JV

Its subsidiary completed a 50% stake acquisition in Advent Convention and Hotels International for a Sahar, Mumbai commercial project with about 1.5 million sq ft leasable area and estimated gross development value of ₹4,500 crore. Long-term development potential is positive, but approvals, construction and leasing remain ahead.

🟠 HCLTech • sovereign AI partnership

Partnered with Cohere to build sovereign AI solutions for enterprises and governments, initially targeting Canada and Germany. The strategic positioning is constructive, but revenue contribution and contract scale have not been quantified. The stock also faces a weak IT-sector tape after Tuesday's decline.

🔴 IIFL Finance • large block supply

Fairfax is offering its 13.25% stake, up to 5.65 crore shares, through a block placement with a ₹611 floor price, around 3.9% below Tuesday's BSE close. The sale is secondary and proceeds go to the seller, not IIFL Finance. The near-term overhang is potential supply and price discovery.

🟢 Senco Gold • festive demand

Reported festive-period retail net sales above ₹2,426 crore, up 26% year-on-year over the 41 days to 11 November; same-store sales growth was 17%. The demand update is positive, but monitor post-festival sales, gold-price effects, margins and inventory.

🔴 Bear • lose 25,269

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Let the opening gap settle

Do not chase the first move. Wait for Nifty to reclaim 25,385 or show a clean rejection below it with volume confirmation.

2 • Bank Nifty is strong but stretched

62,068 is the pivot and 62,648 the first resistance. RSI near 79 warns against buying an extended candle without a defined pullback or base.

3 • Prefer sector leaders

PSU banks, Realty and selected financials led Tuesday. Select stocks that outperform their sector, hold VWAP and show sustained delivery / volume support.

4 • IT needs its own confirmation

Nvidia's beat may improve technology sentiment, but Indian IT services stocks must reclaim their own short-term levels before a reversal can be considered.

5 • Treat broader-market signals carefully

Smallcaps slipped and breadth was almost even. Keep exposure moderate in low-liquidity names and avoid breakouts driven by isolated prints.

6 • Define invalidation before entry

Set the stop, size the position for volatility and keep risk-reward sensible. Avoid averaging down into a failed breakout or carrying oversized exposure into the US policy event.

Final Market View

Positive domestic structure, but do not chase a weak opening.

Tuesday's second consecutive advance and Bank Nifty's record high keep the broader domestic trend constructive. However, Nifty's inability to hold above 25,500, near-even breadth and GIFT Nifty around 25,259 point to a cautious start. Nvidia's strong numbers are a clear positive for global chip sentiment and have lifted US futures, but the regular US session was weak and the 10-year yield remains elevated. Nifty's 25,385 pivot is the immediate balance point, with 25,269 support and 25,503 resistance. Bank Nifty remains stronger, but its stretched momentum argues for disciplined entries. Prefer selective strength only after the opening gap is absorbed and breadth improves; otherwise, let the market consolidate. The key is confirmation, not prediction.

Global Overnight Cues

🟠 Wall Street • mixed finish

Friday's close was uneven: Dow Jones +0.10% at 52,347.55, S&P 500 −0.40% at 7,561.67, Nasdaq Composite −0.91% at 25,136.27 and Russell 2000 −0.31% at 2,790.49. All four major indices still ended the week higher, but technology shares remained the weak pocket.

🔴 AI trade • leadership uncertainty

Technology sentiment remains unsettled after the reported exit of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Nvidia's quarterly results are due Tuesday, 17 November. Investors will focus on forward guidance and AI infrastructure spending, not only the headline earnings beat.

🔴 Asia • softer start

Asian equities edged lower: Japan's Nikkei was down around 0.6%, South Korea's index slipped 0.2% and the broader Asia-Pacific index eased 0.1%. Japan's economy unexpectedly contracted in Q3, adding to concerns about domestic demand. US equity futures were modestly higher in early trade.

🟢 GIFT Nifty • positive opening cue

GIFT Nifty was around 25,263.5 in the morning, roughly 119 points above Friday's Nifty cash close. This indicates a higher start, but the futures premium and the pre-open auction can alter the cash gap. The important test is whether the market holds the opening move after 9:15 IST.

🟠 Rates and global calendar

The US 10-year Treasury yield remains elevated near 5.14%, while markets are pricing further Federal Reserve tightening. US retail-sales data, Nvidia's results on Tuesday and the Fed's October meeting minutes on Wednesday are key events. Trading in Anthropic is expected to begin later in the week, adding another test for AI-sector valuations.

Indian Market Setup

🔴 Friday • benchmark correction

Nifty 50 fell 155.05 points (−0.61%) to 25,144.30; Sensex declined 318.56 points (−0.39%) to 81,592.45. Bank Nifty lost 484.40 points (−0.79%) to 61,034.10. Midcap 100 slipped 0.24% and Smallcap 100 fell 0.26%. Selling was broad enough to make Friday's decline more than a heavyweight-led adjustment.

🔴 Inflation • policy risk

October retail inflation rose to 6.09%, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit. This has revived expectations of a possible rate increase in December. Higher borrowing costs would be a headwind for leveraged businesses and rate-sensitive sectors if bond yields remain elevated.

🟠 Breadth and volatility

Friday's NSE cash-market breadth was negative, with 985 shares advancing, 1,610 declining and 29 unchanged in one broad EQ-series count. India VIX eased 6.75% to 15.50, so the price decline was not accompanied by a volatility spike. A sustainable recovery still needs wider participation.

🟠 Nifty 50 • key levels

Friday's range was 25,054.40–25,246.35, with a close at 25,144.30. The classic pivot is 25,148.35; first support 25,050.35 and first resistance 25,242.30. Above 25,242, the next references are 25,340 and 25,434. Below 25,050, supports sit near 24,956 and 24,858.

🟠 Bank Nifty • recovery must broaden

Bank Nifty closed at 61,034.10 after trading between 60,780.55 and 61,300.55. Its pivot is 61,038.40; first support 60,776.25 and first resistance 61,296.25. A move above 61,296 would improve the tone, while a break below 60,776 exposes 60,518.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🔴 Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles • large quarterly loss

Catalyst: Q2 FY27 consolidated net loss of ₹5,531 crore, against a year-ago profit of ₹76,170 crore; exceptional items were ₹6,738 crore. Revenue rose 34% to ₹96,920 crore and JLR revenue grew 20.3%. Read-through: The exceptional charge is material, but investors will also assess underlying margins, cash flow and JLR demand.

🟢 Eicher Motors • record sales, cost pressure

Catalyst: Q2 revenue rose 20.2% to ₹7,421.72 crore and net profit increased 14.8% to ₹1,573.4 crore. Royal Enfield delivered record quarterly sales of 3,76,722 motorcycles, up 15%. Read-through: Strong demand is supportive, but higher raw-material costs pressured margins and profit missed analyst estimates.

🟢 Ashok Leyland • strong operating quarter

Catalyst: Q2 standalone net profit rose 13% to ₹869 crore, while revenue increased 39% to ₹13,364 crore on stronger commercial-vehicle volumes. Read-through: Monitor whether volume momentum and operating margins sustain through the second half.

🟠 Pricol • Warburg Pincus transaction

Catalyst: Promoters agreed to sell their 38.51% holding in Pricol Autotech to Warburg Pincus for ₹1,663 crore. Read-through: The transaction is strategically important for the planned connected-vehicle business structure; track completion terms and how value is distributed between the listed entity and subsidiary.

🔴 Jubilant Pharmova • sharp profit decline

Catalyst: Q2 consolidated net profit fell 74.1% to ₹31.2 crore even as revenue rose 20.5% to ₹2,370 crore. EBITDA margin narrowed to 10.2% from 17.4%. Read-through: The margin contraction, not just the revenue growth, will drive the market's assessment.

🟠 Yes Bank • AT1 bond legal review

Catalyst: The bank has filed a Supreme Court review petition challenging the October judgment concerning the write-down of ₹8,415 crore in AT1 bonds. Read-through: The legal outcome may affect the bank's capital and legacy-liability narrative; monitor official court developments.

🟢 JSW Infrastructure and BEL • order updates

Catalyst: A JSW Infrastructure subsidiary received a letter of allotment for a cargo terminal project near Rajpura valued at about ₹60 crore. BEL disclosed additional orders worth ₹619 crore across defence electronics and related systems. Read-through: Positive order-flow updates, though execution, margins and project contribution remain the key measures.

🟠 Sandhar Technologies • results due today

Event: Q2 FY27 financial results are scheduled for Monday, 16 November. Watch: Revenue growth, margins, working capital, order pipeline and management commentary. Avoid positioning on an assumed result outcome before the filing.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Treat GIFT as an indication

A higher futures quote does not guarantee a sustained cash-market gap. Wait for the opening range and price acceptance after 9:15 IST.

2 • Use the pivot as a decision point

For Nifty, 25,148 is the balance level, with 25,242 and 25,050 as the first resistance and support. Avoid chasing entries far from the planned invalidation.

3 • Demand broader participation

Friday's breadth was weak. A recovery led by only a few index heavyweights is fragile; monitor advance–decline data and sector confirmation.

4 • Separate earnings from headlines

For Tata Motors PV, Eicher and other result names, assess exceptional items, margins, cash flow and guidance. A headline profit beat or loss alone is not enough.

5 • Respect rates and inflation

With CPI above 6% and WPI due at noon, avoid assuming rate-sensitive stocks will recover simply because the index opens higher.

6 • Size for event risk

Nvidia results and Fed minutes can move global technology and rate expectations. Keep overnight exposure within planned risk limits and avoid averaging into failed breakouts.

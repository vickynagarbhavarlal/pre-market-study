Global Overnight Cues

🟠 US markets • Fed holds, futures soften

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

The global tape suggests a firmer start, but Nifty must reclaim 22,800 and crude remains above $114. Let the first 30–60 minutes show whether price holds above resistance or rejects it. IPO deadlines and earnings are event risks, not chart setups.

Use higher timeframes

Start monthly, then weekly, then daily charts; use the 2-hour chart as the lowest timeframe. Prioritise relative strength versus Nifty, clean bases or breakout-retests, supportive volume and a clear invalidation level. Avoid forcing a trade because the opening gap looks attractive.

Define risk first

Set stop-loss and position size before entry. If volatility widens the stop distance, reduce size rather than loosening the stop. Check earnings dates, liquidity and overnight-gap risk; do not buy solely because IPO GMP is high or a headline sounds bullish.

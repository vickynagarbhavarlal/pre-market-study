01

Global Overnight Cues

🔴 US markets • AI trade under pressure

Wall Street fell on Thursday, 29 October: Dow −0.25% to 52,342.56, S&P 500 −0.30% to 7,720.39 and Nasdaq −1.37% to 26,184.72. Alphabet plunged 12.6% after a revenue miss and heavier AI spending; semiconductor shares lost 1.4%, while Amazon fell 6.5% in after-hours trade on weak sales guidance. The Russell 2000 gained 1.0%, so the sell-off was concentrated in large technology rather than a uniform liquidation. US Q3 GDP grew 3.4%, but inflation pressure remained elevated. The Fed held rates at 3.75%–4.00% on Wednesday in a 10–2 vote.

🔴 Asia • technology weakness weighs

Friday’s early Asian tone is defensive and divergent. South Korea’s KOSPI fell more than 3% as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix weakened; the MSCI Asia-Pacific index slipped 0.3%. S&P 500 futures were down 0.8%, Nikkei 225 futures fell 1.8%, and Japan’s Topix was down 0.4%; Hang Seng futures rose 0.4% and Australia’s ASX 200 gained 1.1%. The Bank of Japan is due to announce policy today; Reuters expects rates to remain at 1.25%, but a hawkish tone and the market’s pricing of a possible December hike could move the yen and global yields.

🔴 Crude • rebound keeps India exposed

Brent rose about 0.5% to $115.44/bbl and WTI gained 0.8% to $102.79 in early Asian trade on Friday after settling lower on hopes of US–Iran talks. Supply and shipping risks remain unresolved; Brent is on course for an 11.5% monthly gain and a fourth consecutive month higher. This is still an adverse input-cost backdrop for an oil-importing market such as India.

Scenario Map

🟢 Bullish continuation

If Nifty holds above 22,800 and reclaims 23,047 with breadth, the next calculated reference is 23,157, followed by the 23,175 technical resistance. A sustained break above 23,175 would strengthen the path toward 23,300. Bank Nifty above 55,244 would add confirmation. Require acceptance, not just an intraday spike.

🟠 Consolidation

If Nifty holds 22,800 but struggles below 23,047–23,175, a range between roughly 22,800 and 23,050 is plausible. The positive domestic breadth and DII buying may cushion declines, while US tech weakness, BOJ headlines and the earnings calendar can create sharp two-way moves.

🔴 Rebound failure

A sustained break below 22,742 exposes 22,547; losing the 22,800 zone would be an early warning. Bank Nifty below 54,312 opens 53,714. Renewed crude strength, another rupee sell-off or deeper global technology weakness would reinforce the downside case. Reassess if Nifty regains 23,047 with broad participation.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase the opening gap

GIFT Nifty signals a muted start, while US technology and Asian chip stocks are weak. Let the first 30–60 minutes establish whether Nifty can hold 22,800 and whether Bank Nifty confirms. A strong previous close does not guarantee a strong next session.

Keep the higher-timeframe sequence

Start with monthly, then weekly, then daily charts; use the 2-hour chart as the lowest timeframe. Prioritise relative strength versus Nifty, clean bases, breakout-retests, supportive volume and a clear invalidation point. Prefer a small number of well-formed setups over broad watchlist activity.

Price the event risk

Check earnings dates, block deals, ex-dates, liquidity and overnight gap risk before entry. Set stop-loss and position size first; reduce size when volatility widens the stop distance. Do not buy solely because a result headline is positive or an IPO GMP is elevated.

Final Market View

Domestic recovery intact • global risk-off • selective execution

Thursday’s +1.05% Nifty rise, +1.24% Sensex advance, 1.59 advance/decline ratio and VIX decline to 15.12 show that the domestic rebound had breadth. DII buying of ₹1,924 Cr absorbed FII selling of ₹944 Cr. But the overnight hand-off is not supportive: Nasdaq fell 1.37%, Asian chip shares are weak and Brent is back near $115. GIFT Nifty at 23,028 indicates a muted start rather than a decisive fresh impulse. The key line for Nifty is 22,800; resistance is 23,047–23,175. Bank Nifty must clear 55,244 to strengthen the banking confirmation.

Practical stance: stay selective and let price confirm. A sustained move above 23,175 with breadth supports continuation; a break below 22,800 shifts focus to 22,742 and 22,547. Heavy results, the BOJ decision and Sagility’s reported block deal can create stock-specific gaps. Avoid forcing fresh swing trades before levels, volume and risk are clear.

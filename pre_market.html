TODAY'S CORE MESSAGE

Do not mistake the two-day bounce for a trend reversal.

Nifty recovered 0.43% on Wednesday, but the index remains well below its recent swing high and FIIs continued selling. The Fed's 25-bps hike and guidance for possible further tightening raise the bar for sustained risk-on flows. GIFT Nifty is almost flat-to-marginally positive, so there is no strong pre-open confirmation. Holding 23,100–23,200 keeps the rebound alive; above 23,300, 23,375 and 23,465 become the next upside tests. Below 23,100, the market can retest 23,000 and 22,960.

05

Sector Radar

🟢 Financials

Bank Nifty gained 0.89% Wednesday and remains the clearest large-cap support pocket. The trade improves only if banks hold above Wednesday's close and break resistance.

🟢 IT / AI

U.S. tech was comparatively resilient, while Indian IT had recently benefited from valuation and AI sentiment. Higher U.S. yields remain the key valuation risk.

🟠 Auto

Auto can benefit from selective short covering as crude cools, but the sector still faces demand and input-cost sensitivity if oil reverses higher.

🟠 Pharma

Defensive earnings and stock-specific regulatory developments can provide relative stability if the index remains range-bound.

🔴 Metals / oil users

Higher global rates and still-elevated crude keep pressure on rate-sensitive and energy-intensive businesses. Avoid broad sector bets without relative strength.

🟠 Mid / Small caps

Short-covering can produce sharp moves, but the risk-reward is poor when the index has not reclaimed major resistance. Position size matters.

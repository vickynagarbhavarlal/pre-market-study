10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Let the opening range form. A gap-up without breadth or bank confirmation is not a swing entry signal.

Use higher timeframes

Start with monthly and weekly trends, then daily structure; use the 2-hour chart to refine entries. An intraday bounce alone is not a trend reversal.

Define risk first

Prioritise liquid stocks with relative strength and verifiable catalysts. Define invalidation, size to stop distance and avoid averaging down into broken structures.

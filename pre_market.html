Scenario Map

🟢 Relief bounce holds

If Nifty defends 22,370–22,400, reclaims 22,745 and sustains above 22,800–22,900 with improving breadth, a move toward 23,000 is possible. Treat this as a counter-trend recovery until higher highs and stronger sector participation appear.

🟠 Range / whipsaw

Between support near 22,370 and resistance around 22,800–22,900, two-way trade and failed breakouts are plausible. Reduce size, avoid chasing the first candle and wait for a close/hold or retest.

🔴 Support breaks

A decisive move below 22,370 opens the 22,180–22,200 zone; below that, 22,000 becomes a psychological reference. A weak Bank Nifty below 53,759–53,359 would reinforce risk-off conditions. These are conditional levels, not predictions.

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Wait for confirmation

Do not turn a weak opening into an automatic short or a gap-down into an automatic buy. Let the first 30–60 minutes reveal whether price accepts above/below key levels; use daily and 2-hour structure rather than reacting to every intraday tick.

Use higher timeframes

Start with monthly, then weekly, then daily charts. Prefer stocks showing relative strength against Nifty, a clear base or breakout-retest, supportive volume and a defined invalidation level. Avoid chasing a stock solely because it appears in the news.

Define risk first

Set stop-loss and position size before entry. In a high-volatility, event-heavy week, reduce exposure when the stop distance widens, avoid averaging down into invalidation, and check liquidity, earnings dates and overnight-gap risk.

Weak structure • cautious opening • selective only

Base case: cautious-to-weak start, with elevated crude, negative global cues and a bearish broader technical structure. Friday's FII/DII buying is supportive but does not negate the four-session decline.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not chase the opening print

GIFT is only modestly positive after a strong weekly rally. Let the first 15–30 minutes establish direction and avoid buying an opening spike without a nearby invalidation.

2 • Respect overbought momentum

Nifty RSI 77.42 and Bank Nifty RSI 72.13 are stretched. Strong trends can stay overbought, but fresh entries need better reward-to-risk than simply buying strength.

3 • Treat Muhurat pivots cautiously

Sunday's 70-minute session gives a thin sample for classical pivots and volume comparisons. Use them as reference zones alongside Friday's full-session levels and Monday's opening range.

4 • Expiry is not a directional signal

Monday's shifted Nifty expiry can amplify gamma, pinning and abrupt reversals. Avoid oversized option positions and verify the correct expiry contract.

5 • Prefer disclosed catalysts

Look for earnings surprises, exchange filings and sector-relative strength backed by volume. Separate consolidated from standalone results and read exceptional items before reacting.

6 • Holiday-aware exposure

Tuesday is closed for cash and equity derivatives. Account for overnight gap risk, settlement and broker-ledger availability before carrying positions through the closure.

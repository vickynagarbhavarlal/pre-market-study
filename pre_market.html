Overnight Global Cues

Asian markets are higher, but the US close was mixed and technology shares remained under pressure.

🇺🇸 US markets • mixed close

Wednesday's final session before Thanksgiving ended mixed. Dow Jones gained 0.33% to 50,182.95, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.25% to 7,182.78 and Nasdaq Composite fell 0.62% to 23,585.80. The intraday technology selloff was considerably sharper before a partial recovery; Nvidia ended about 4% lower. US cash equity markets are closed today for Thanksgiving, limiting fresh US price discovery.

🌏 Asia • positive early trade

Japan's Nikkei 225 was up around 1.21% and Topix 0.71% in early trade. South Korea's Kospi gained about 0.90% and Kosdaq 1.62%. Hong Kong futures were above the previous Hang Seng close. The regional rebound offers support to the opening, although technology and AI-related valuation concerns have not disappeared.

📈 GIFT Nifty • higher opening indication

GIFT Nifty traded near 26,082, up about 157 points or 0.60% in the morning snapshot. The futures indication points to a gap-up start relative to Wednesday's Nifty close of 25,819.25. Futures basis, pre-open orders and overseas moves can change before 09:15 IST; this is an indication, not a guaranteed cash opening level.

🛢️ Crude oil • elevated near $95

Brent crude was around $94.56 a barrel, marginally lower by 0.06%, while WTI traded near $84, up about 0.38%. Prices have held recent gains amid supply-disruption concerns. The higher absolute level matters for India's import bill, inflation and fuel-sensitive sectors even if the latest percentage move is small.

💱 Rupee • eight-month high

The rupee strengthened to an eight-month high of around ₹92.1850 per US dollar intraday on Wednesday and closed near ₹92.23. The improvement has been supported by foreign equity inflows and currency demand-supply conditions. The dollar index was around 103.73, slightly firmer. Watch whether the rupee holds its recent gains as crude remains elevated.

📊 Bond yields • lower US, stable India

The US 10-year Treasury yield was near 4.85% at Wednesday's close after easing as investors sought safety during the equity selloff. India's 10-year government bond yield was around 7.16–7.18%. Lower global yields can ease pressure on emerging markets, but inflation and tariff uncertainty remain important drivers.

⚠️ Trade policy • proposed US tariffs

The United States has outlined a proposed tariff framework that includes a 17% rate for India, with higher proposed rates for some other trading partners. These are proposals, not a confirmed final tariff schedule. Any escalation could affect export-oriented sectors, currency expectations and global risk appetite; monitor official details rather than trading headlines alone.

Stock-Specific News & Catalysts

🟢 Siemens Energy India • strong results

Reported standalone quarterly net profit of ₹653 crore, up 82% year on year, on revenue of ₹3,453 crore, up 30.5%. EBITDA rose 72% to ₹822 crore and margin expanded to 23.8%. Strong operating momentum is positive; monitor order execution, working capital and valuation expectations.

🟢 Diamond Power Infrastructure • ₹488 crore orders

Received two purchase orders worth ₹488.18 crore including GST from Adani Energy Solutions for AL-59 conductors used in a major high-voltage transmission project. The order adds visibility, but delivery schedules, working-capital needs and margin conversion remain important.

🟢 Juniper Green Energy • 150 MW hybrid PPA

Its subsidiary signed a 25-year power purchase agreement with NTPC for a 150 MW wind-solar hybrid project. The filing indicates annual revenue potential of around ₹155 crore, with scheduled supply commencement in June 2028. This improves long-term revenue visibility; execution is still ahead.

🟢 Lupin • USFDA approval

Received USFDA approval for Dalbavancin Injection, 500 mg/5 mL, through the 505(b)(2) pathway for specified acute bacterial skin and skin-structure infections. The approval expands its injectable portfolio; launch timing, competition and commercial uptake will determine the earnings impact.

🟢 Wipro • seven-year bp partnership

Announced a strategic partnership to transform bp's core finance operations, with around 3,000 bp employees across India, Hungary and Malaysia expected to transition to Wipro. The long-duration engagement may improve business visibility; contract economics, transition costs and margins need monitoring.

🟢 Adani Power • rating upgrade

Received an upgrade in its credit rating to AA+/Stable from AA/Stable for enhanced bank-loan and non-convertible-debenture facilities. The improved rating is constructive for funding access, although leverage, cash flows and project execution remain the core variables.

🟢 Jupiter Wagons • metro-coach joint venture

Entered an agreement with South Korea's Woojin Industrial Systems, with Jupiter Wagons holding an 80% stake. The venture aims to manufacture complete metro coaches and propulsion systems in India. This opens a new product opportunity, but execution, investment and order conversion will take time.

🟠 Adani Enterprises • airport subsidiary funding

Adani Airport Holdings entered a binding agreement to raise ₹2,790 crore from funds and affiliates managed by 360 ONE Alternates. The funding supports the airport subsidiary's capital base and expansion plans; it is not an immediate earnings upgrade for the listed parent.

🟠 Alembic Pharmaceuticals • inspection reports

Received two Establishment Inspection Reports for its Vadodara bioequivalence facility, closing the inspection conducted from 31 August to 10 September. The update is constructive from a regulatory standpoint. Continue to monitor future inspections and compliance status.

🟠 Tata Steel and APL Apollo • block transaction

Tata Steel acquired about 2.77 crore APL Apollo Tubes shares, worth roughly ₹5,825 crore, from a promoter-group entity at ₹2,100 per share. The transaction may have strategic implications, but APL Apollo remained volatile and fell 11.62% on Wednesday. Watch supply, ownership changes and price acceptance rather than assuming an automatic positive reaction.

🔴 LG Electronics India • promoter stake sale

The promoter is looking to sell up to 2% of the company's equity through a block deal, with a floor price of ₹1,709.30, around a 6% discount to the last close. The transaction may create near-term supply pressure; final size and institutional demand will determine the price impact.

🔴 Schaeffler India • parent stake sale proposal

Its parent is reported to be considering a sale of up to 6% stake, with an indicative floor price of ₹3,400, around a 5.6% discount to the last close. A large block can weigh on near-term price discovery even if operating fundamentals are unchanged.

🔴 Lenskart Solutions • foreign investor selling

Two foreign institutional investors sold around 1.27 crore shares worth approximately ₹920.58 crore through block deals at ₹725 per share. Institutional absorption and subsequent delivery data will matter; a large secondary sale can create an overhang in the near term.

🔴 Polyplex Corporation • Thailand line closure

Its Thai subsidiary approved mothballing or closure of a CPP production line. The impact will depend on capacity utilisation, restructuring costs and the ability to redeploy production. Assess the financial contribution of the affected line before drawing conclusions.

🟠 Hyundai Motor India • price revision

Announced price increases of up to ₹8,000 across select models and trims from 1 December, citing higher input costs. The move may partly offset cost inflation, but the scale of pass-through and demand elasticity will determine the margin benefit.

Swing-Trader Framework

1 • Do not chase the opening gap

GIFT Nifty points to a positive start, but the index is approaching the 26,000 resistance zone. Let the first 15–30 minutes establish the opening range. A gap that loses its opening-range low and VWAP is a warning, not a reason to average down.

2 • Use the pivot as the balance line

Nifty 25,797 is the central reference, with 25,974 as R1 and 25,642 as S1. Trade only when price action, VWAP and breadth support the same direction. A level by itself is not an entry signal.

3 • Demand banking confirmation

Bank Nifty needs to sustain above 64,142 to strengthen the recovery. A rejection near that level or a break below 63,256 would warn that financial-sector participation is fading.

4 • Do not ignore negative breadth

Wednesday's broad-market advance/decline ratio was only 0.74 despite strong benchmark gains. Focus on stocks showing independent relative strength; avoid indiscriminate buying in mid- and small-cap names.

5 • Treat block deals as supply events

LG Electronics India, Schaeffler India and Lenskart have reported stake-sale activity. Avoid interpreting a discount block price as automatic support; observe actual institutional absorption and post-deal price behaviour.

6 • Keep risk proportional to volatility

India VIX remains near 17.7. Set the stop before entry, size positions according to the stop distance and avoid oversized overnight exposure. A missed trade costs nothing; a poorly sized gap risk can damage capital.

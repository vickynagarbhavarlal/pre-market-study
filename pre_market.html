FINAL MARKET VIEW

Cautiously positive structure, but Monday is a confirmation session—not a chase session.

Nifty has recovered for three sessions and FII/DII flows turned supportive on Friday. Lower crude is another positive. But GIFT Nifty points to a cautious opening, U.S. yields remain near 5%, and the index is still below important medium-term averages. Above 23,400–23,450, the recovery gains credibility; below 23,250, the market can slip toward 23,100 and 23,000.

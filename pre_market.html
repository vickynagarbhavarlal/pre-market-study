Don't predict the candle. React to what price actually proves.

🟠 Range scenario

09

For Swing Traders

The important part

Don't buy the opening. Buy the evidence.

Friday's close was only 0.08% higher and the weekly index performance remained negative. That means there is no reason to front-run a breakout. A swing setup becomes more interesting if Nifty breaks 24,284–24,300, holds the zone, and sector breadth confirms the move. Conversely, rejection there is information too — it tells you the market is still range-bound.

FINAL MARKET VIEW • 24 AUGUST 2026

Mildly positive opening. The real test starts at 24,284.

Global equities ended Friday higher and GIFT Nifty points to a positive start, but crude near $93.43 and elevated bond yields prevent a clean bullish call. The most useful framework today is simple: above 24,284–24,300, recovery gains credibility; inside 24,200–24,284, expect range behaviour; below 24,000, caution returns sharply.

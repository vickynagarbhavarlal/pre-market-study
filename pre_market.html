Q2 profit rose 7.6% YoY to ₹13,884 Cr and revenue rose 11.2% to ₹73,188 Cr, but EBIT margin fell to 23.98% from 25.17%; dividend ₹12/share announced. Why it matters: growth and margin quality matter more than headline profit amid weak IT sentiment.

US regulatory action affecting access to a green-card-linked labour-certification programme adds uncertainty for Indian IT outsourcing firms. Why it matters: staffing flexibility, client delivery and sector sentiment could be affected; avoid treating the whole sector as a uniform trade.

🟢 JSW Steel — production

10

Swing-Trader Framework

Don't chase the gap

Wait for the first 30–60 minutes to establish range, breadth and whether price holds above the opening zone. A gap-up after a selloff can fail quickly.

Trade only defined setups

Use daily and weekly structure, a clear invalidation level, position sizing from stop distance and a minimum acceptable reward/risk. No averaging down in a broken chart.

Protect capital first

When the index is below key trend levels and institutional flows are negative, reduce exposure and raise the bar for fresh longs. A watchlist is not an obligation to trade.

Friday is not a dip-buying setup by default. Let 23,300–23,400 prove itself before taking risk.

Stocks in Focus

Named stocks with verified company-specific triggers, separated from broad market interpretation.

🟢 BULLISH

HDFC Bank

Trigger: Bahrain High Civil Court rejected the final two investor proceedings linked to Credit Suisse AT1 bonds, taking the total favourable rulings to seven. Why it matters: removes a material legal overhang and reinforces the bank's defence against mis-selling allegations.

🟢 BULLISH

Shakti Pumps

Trigger: MSEDCL awarded/empanelled the company for 10,000 off-grid solar water-pumping systems worth about ₹235.92 Cr including GST, to be executed within 60 days of work order/NTP. Why it matters: meaningful order-book visibility and direct exposure to state solar-pump demand.

🟢 BULLISH

IRB Infrastructure

Trigger: August toll revenue rose about 25% YoY, with major corridors including Mumbai–Pune showing strong growth. Why it matters: sustained traffic growth plus tariff revisions support cash-flow visibility across its toll portfolio.

🟢 BULLISH

ONGC / Oil India

Trigger: upstream stocks strengthened as Brent moved above $100; ONGC gained 1.39% on Thursday and Oil India also attracted brokerage attention. Why it matters: higher crude can support upstream realisations, although the broader market impact of expensive oil is negative for India.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

HCLTech

Trigger: Citi retained Sell with a ₹1,110 target, citing limited re-rating scope, macro pressure and wage/investment costs despite progress in advanced AI. Why it matters: the stock closed down about 1.85% on Thursday and remains vulnerable if global risk-off pressure persists.

🔴 BEARISH / RISK

Indian IT

Trigger: U.S. policy uncertainty around H-1B visa-holder grace periods adds another labour-mobility risk while higher U.S. yields pressure technology valuations. Why it matters: the sector is sensitive to both valuation compression and U.S. client demand/mobility assumptions.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

ICICI Prudential AMC

Trigger: RBI approved the AMC to acquire up to 9.95% in four banks including Kotak Mahindra Bank. Why it matters: expands investment flexibility, but the approval itself is not an earnings event; monitor eventual stake-building.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

NSE

Trigger: NSE's IPO size was trimmed to about 5.2% of equity with a ₹1,700–1,785 price band; subscription is planned for 17–21 September. Why it matters: the IPO is a major market event, while reduced offer size reflects investor sensitivity to valuation and derivatives-regulation changes.

🟠 NEUTRAL / WATCH

Redington

Trigger: shares rallied after Apple's iPhone 18 Pro-series launch; Redington is an authorised distributor in India. Why it matters: product-cycle demand can support near-term distribution volumes, but the move is event-driven and should be separated from longer-term fundamentals.

09

Swing-Trader Framework

Long setup

Reclaim, don't anticipate

Wait for 23,500–23,600 to be reclaimed and held with breadth. A falling knife during an oil shock is not a high-quality swing entry.

Short setup

Below 23,300

If 23,300 breaks with breadth and weak financials, favour relative-strength shorts with defined stops. Do not average into a breakdown.

Risk control

Smaller size

Crude near $110, U.S. yields near 5% and geopolitical headlines create gap risk. Reduce leverage and avoid oversized overnight positions.

FINAL MARKET VIEW

Bearish at the open; 23,300–23,400 must hold to change the tone.

Friday's opening setup is materially worse than Thursday's close suggests. GIFT Nifty is lower, Brent is near $110, the rupee is under pressure and global yields have surged. The first objective is not to predict a rebound; it is to see whether buyers defend 23,300–23,400. A sustained reclaim of 23,600 would be the first meaningful repair, while 23,800 remains the stronger reversal confirmation.

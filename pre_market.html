10

Swing-Trader Framework

Do not chase a muted open

Allow the first 30–60 minutes to define direction. Confirm whether Nifty holds 23,100–23,150 and whether banks participate before acting on a bullish bias.

Keep higher timeframes in control

Start from monthly and weekly structure, then daily charts. One recovery week does not automatically reverse the broader trend. Define invalidation before entry and size positions to stop distance.

Respect earnings and oil risk

Bank results can create gaps; crude and currency can shift sentiment quickly. Avoid oversized positions, averaging down into broken setups or using IPO GMP as a dependable return forecast.

Final Market View

Neutral opening • Selective opportunities

GIFT Nifty around 23,131 at 7:49 AM indicated a muted start after Nifty closed at 23,146.15. Last week’s 2.78% Nifty gain and improving large-cap breadth are constructive, while ICICI Bank results are supportive. Watch 23,100–23,150 as the opening pivot and 23,250 as an upside confirmation area; a break below 23,000 would weaken the recovery. Prefer liquid, catalyst-backed swing setups with defined risk.

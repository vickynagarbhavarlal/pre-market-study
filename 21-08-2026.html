For Swing Traders

The important part

Don't chase yesterday's rebound.

Yesterday's +0.64% recovery is encouraging, but it doesn't erase the recent weakness. For a swing trader, the better signal is a market that starts building higher lows and successfully reclaims resistance. A gap-up followed by immediate selling is not bullish confirmation. A breakout followed by sustained acceptance is. Let the market prove the thesis before increasing exposure.
